健康饮食的小习惯

健康饮食的小习惯

一、英文原文

Small Habits for Healthy Eating

Healthy eating is important for our body. Good food gives us energy every day. We can start with small and simple changes.

Breakfast is the most important meal. It helps our brain work better at school. We should eat breakfast every morning.

Fruits and vegetables are full of vitamins. They help our body fight sickness. Try to eat five servings each day.

Water is the best drink for our health. It keeps our body clean and cool. We should drink eight glasses every day.

Too much sugar is not good for us. It can hurt our teeth and make us tired. Choose natural foods like apples instead.

Cooking at home is a great habit. We can control what goes into our meals. It is also fun to cook with family.

Healthy eating does not have to be difficult. Small changes can make a big difference. Start today and feel the benefits.

（共 175 词）

二、逐句精读

第一段

Healthy eating is important for our body.

健康饮食对我们的身体很重要。

Good food gives us energy every day.

好的食物每天给我们能量。

We can start with small and simple changes.

我们可以从微小而简单的改变开始。

单词词性释义
energyn.能量，精力

第二段

早餐是最重要的一餐。

它帮助我们的大脑在学校里更好地运作。

我们应该每天早上吃早餐。

Fruits and vegetables are full of vitamins.

水果和蔬菜富含维生素。

They help our body fight sickness.

它们帮助我们的身体对抗疾病。

试着每天吃五份。

选择苹果这样的天然食物来代替。

在家做饭是一个很好的习惯。

三、语法简析

1. 形容词作表语（主系表结构）

形容词放在 be 动词后面作表语，描述主语的状态或特征。

原句： Healthy eating is important for our body.

结构： 主语 + be 动词 + 形容词（+ 介词短语）

练习造句：

  • Reading is useful for students.（阅读对学生很有用。）
  • Exercise is good for our health.（运动对我们的健康有益。）

2. 情态动词 should 表示建议

should 表示"应该"，后接动词原形，用于给出建议或提出忠告。

原句： We should eat breakfast every morning.

结构： 主语 + should + 动词原形

练习造句：

  • You should drink more water every day.（你应该每天多喝水。）
  • Students should do their homework on time.（学生应该按时做作业。）

3. too much + 不可数名词 表示"太多"

too much 修饰不可数名词，表示数量过多，常带有负面含义。

原句： Too much sugar is not good for us.

结构： too much + 不可数名词 + 谓语 + ...

练习造句：

  • Too much noise is bad for sleep.（太多噪音对睡眠不好。）
  • Too much homework makes students stressed.（太多作业让学生感到压力大。）

四、阅读理解

1. Why is breakfast important?

A. It tastes very good.
B. It helps our brain work better.
C. It is cheap to make.
D. It has no sugar.

2. How many servings of fruits and vegetables should we eat each day?

A. Two
B. Three
C. Four
D. Five

3. What is the best drink for our health?

A. Juice
B. Milk
C. Water
D. Coffee

4. Why is cooking at home a good habit?

A. It is faster than eating out.
B. We can control what goes into our meals.
C. It costs no money.
D. We do not need to wash dishes.

答案：1.B 2.D 3.C 4.B
