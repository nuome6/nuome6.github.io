睡眠的魔力

Nuouo2026/6/7大约 5 分钟英语学习每日英语基础英语

睡眠的魔力

一、英文原文

The Magic of Sleep

Sleep is very important for our health and well-being. It helps our body rest and recover from a long day. A good night of sleep makes us feel energized and ready for the morning.

When we sleep, our brain works very hard all night. It sorts through the day's information and organizes it. This helps us remember things better in the morning.

Children need more sleep than grown adults do. Most children need nine to eleven hours of sleep each night. Adults usually need seven to nine hours for good health.

A good sleep routine helps us sleep better. We should go to bed at the same time each night. We should also wake up at the same time each morning.

Drinking coffee before bed can keep us awake for many hours. We should stop drinking coffee in the afternoon or evening. Warm milk is a much better choice before we go to sleep.

Exercise during the day can improve our sleep. But we should not exercise right before bed. Our body needs time to calm down.

A dark and quiet room is very good for our sleep. We should turn off all the lights and our phones at night. Good sleep prepares us for a happy and productive new day.

（共 215 词）

一夜好眠让我们感到充满活力。

三、语法简析

一般现在时表示事实和习惯

本文大量使用一般现在时，用于表达客观事实和日常习惯。

原句： Sleep is very important for our health.

结构： 主语（Sleep）+ 系动词（is）+ 表语（very important）+ 补充说明（for our health）。一般现在时描述普遍真理。

练习造句：

  • 水在零摄氏度结冰。（Water freezes at zero degrees.）
  • 我每天早上六点起床。（I wake up at six every morning.）

"should" 表示建议

"should" 是情态动词，表示建议或应该做的事情，后面接动词原形。

原句： We should go to bed at the same time each night.

结构： 主语（We）+ 情态动词（should）+ 动词原形（go）+ 状语（to bed...）。"should" 比 "must" 语气更温和。

练习造句：

  • 你应该多喝水。（You should drink more water.）
  • 他们应该每天运动。（They should exercise every day.）

比较级 "more ... than"

"more + 形容词 + than" 结构用于比较两个事物的不同程度。

原句： Children need more sleep than adults.

结构： 主语（Children）+ 谓语（need）+ 比较级宾语（more sleep）+ 比较连词（than）+ 比较对象（adults）。注意这里的 "more" 是 "much" 的比较级形式。

练习造句：

  • 跑步比走路消耗更多能量。（Running needs more energy than walking.）
  • 我比你高。（I am taller than you.）

四、阅读理解

1. What helps our body rest and recover?

A. Coffee
B. Exercise
C. Sleep
D. Warm milk

2. How many hours of sleep do most children need?

A. Five to seven hours
B. Seven to nine hours
C. Nine to eleven hours
D. Eleven to thirteen hours

3. What should we NOT do before bed?

A. Drink warm milk
B. Turn off lights
C. Exercise
D. Go to bed at the same time

4. What kind of room is good for sleep?

A. Bright and noisy
B. Dark and quiet
C. Bright and quiet
D. Hot and noisy

答案：1.C 2.C 3.C 4.B
