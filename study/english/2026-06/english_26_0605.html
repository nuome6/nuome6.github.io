学习的好方法

学习的好方法

一、英文原文

Good Ways to Study

Studying is a very important part of our daily lives at school. Good study habits can help us learn new things better. Every student can improve with the right methods and tools.

It is a good idea to make a study plan every single week. Choose a regular time to sit down and study each day. This simple habit helps your brain get ready to learn each time.

You should try to find a quiet place to do your study. Your desk needs to be clean and tidy at all times. Turn off your phone during your study time at home.

Taking good notes is a very helpful way to learn. Write down the most important points from each lesson. Use different colors to highlight the most important ideas.

Do not be afraid to ask questions in your class. Your teachers are happy to explain things one more time. You can also ask your classmates for help and ideas.

Your brain also needs some time to rest and relax. Take a short break after every hour of hard study. Stand up and stretch your body for a few minutes.

Review what you have learned at the end of each day. Practice makes your knowledge much stronger over time. Good study skills will help you for a whole lifetime.

（共 222 词）

三、语法简析

情态动词 can 的用法

情态动词 can 表示"能够、可以"，后面直接跟动词原形，没有人称和数的变化。

原句： Good study habits can help us learn new things better.

结构： 主语 + can + 动词原形 + 宾语。can 不随主语变化，否定形式为 cannot/can't。

练习造句：

  • I can finish my homework before dinner.（我可以在晚饭前完成作业。）
  • She can read English books very well.（她英文书读得很好。）

祈使句的用法

祈使句用来表达命令、建议或请求，以动词原形开头，主语 you 通常省略。

原句： Choose a regular time to sit down and study each day.

结构： 动词原形 + 宾语/状语。否定祈使句在句首加 Don't。

练习造句：

  • Write down your ideas in a notebook.（把你的想法写在笔记本里。）
  • Don't forget to review your lessons every day.（别忘了每天复习功课。）

动词不定式作目的状语

to + 动词原形构成不定式，可以表示做某事的目的，放在主句后面。

原句： Take a short break after every hour of hard study.

结构： 主语 + 谓语 + 宾语 + to + 动词原形。to 后面的动词表示目的。

练习造句：

  • I go to the library to read books every weekend.（我每周末去图书馆看书。）
  • We practice English every day to improve our speaking.（我们每天练习英语来提高口语。）

四、阅读理解

1. What is the article mainly about?

A. How to make friends at school
B. Good ways to study and learn
C. The importance of eating healthy food
D. How to save money every week

2. According to the article, what should you do during study time?

A. Watch videos on your phone
B. Talk to your friends loudly
C. Turn off your phone
D. Eat snacks at your desk

3. Why is it good to take a break while studying?

A. The brain needs some time to rest
B. The teacher tells you to stop
C. Your phone needs charging
D. The study plan is finished

4. What does "Practice makes your knowledge stronger" mean?

A. Practicing helps you learn better
B. Practice is not important
C. You should only study once
D. Knowledge comes from sleeping

答案：1.B 2.C 3.A 4.A
