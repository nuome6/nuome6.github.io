友情的重要性

Nuouo2026/6/1大约 5 分钟英语学习每日英语基础英语

友情的重要性

一、英文原文

The Importance of Friendship

Friendship is a special and important bond between people. Good friends share happy moments and sad times together. True friends make our lives more meaningful and fun.

We need friends to share our feelings and inner thoughts. They help us deal with problems and worries in life. Friends make us feel less lonely during hard times.

True friends always support us in difficult moments. They do not judge us when we make mistakes or fail. They accept us completely for who we really are.

A good friend is also a careful listener. They pay close attention to everything we say. Honest friends always tell us the truth with kindness.

Making new friends often starts with a simple greeting. A warm smile and hello can open many doors. Sharing hobbies and common interests helps build strong connections.

Keeping a friendship strong takes real effort from both people. Good friends always find time for each other no matter what. They keep their promises and stay loyal through everything.

Friends bring joy and warm laughter into our daily lives. They help us feel less lonely and more confident about ourselves. Having close and caring friends is truly good for our health.

（共 204 词）

二、逐句精读

第一段

Friendship is a special and important bond between people.

友谊是人与人之间一种特殊而重要的纽带。

Good friends share happy moments and sad times together.

好朋友一起分享快乐的时光和悲伤的时刻。

True friends make our lives more meaningful and fun.

真正的朋友让我们的生活更有意义、更有趣。

单词词性释义
friendshipn.友谊
bondn.纽带，联系
meaningfuladj.有意义的

第二段

We need friends to share our feelings and inner thoughts.

我们需要朋友来分享我们的感受和内心想法。

They help us deal with problems and worries in life.

他们帮助我们处理生活中的问题和烦恼。

Friends make us feel less lonely during hard times.

朋友让我们在困难时期感到不那么孤独。

单词词性释义
feelingsn.感受，情感
deal withphr.处理，应对
lonelyadj.孤独的

第三段

True friends always support us in difficult moments.

真正的朋友总是在困难时刻支持我们。

They do not judge us when we make mistakes or fail.

当我们犯错或失败时，他们不会评判我们。

They accept us completely for who we really are.

他们完全接受真实的我们。

单词词性释义
supportv.支持
judgev.评判，判断
acceptv.接受

第四段

A good friend is also a careful listener.

好朋友也是一个细心的倾听者。

They pay close attention to everything we say.

他们密切关注我们说的每一句话。

Honest friends always tell us the truth with kindness.

诚实的朋友总是友善地告诉我们真相。

单词词性释义

honestadj.诚实的

第五段

结交新朋友通常从一个简单的问候开始。

一个温暖的微笑和问候可以打开许多扇门。

分享爱好和共同兴趣有助于建立牢固的联系。

维持一段坚固的友谊需要双方付出真正的努力。

好朋友无论如何都会为彼此腾出时间。

他们信守承诺，无论发生什么都保持忠诚。

They help us feel less lonely and more confident about ourselves.

他们帮助我们减少孤独感，对自己更有信心。

三、语法简析

1. "形容词 + 名词" 搭配

英语中形容词放在名词前面，用来描述名词的特征。这是最基础的语序规则。

原句： Good friends share happy moments and sad times together.

结构： 形容词 + 名词（good friends, happy moments, sad times）

练习造句：

  • The tall boy is my brother.（那个高个子男孩是我的哥哥。）
  • She has a beautiful smile.（她有一个美丽的微笑。）

2. help + 宾语 + 动词原形

help 后面接宾语再接动词时，动词用原形，不需要加 to。

原句： They help us deal with problems and worries in life.

结构： help + 宾语（us）+ 动词原形（deal）

练习造句：

  • She helps me learn English every day.（她每天帮我学英语。）
  • The teacher helps students understand the lesson.（老师帮助学生理解课程。）

3. start with 短语

"start with" 表示"以……开始"，常用于描述做某事的第一步。

原句： Making new friends often starts with a simple greeting.

结构： 主语 + start with + 名词/动名词

练习造句：

  • The class starts with a short song.（课堂以一首短歌开始。）
  • Let's start with the first question.（让我们从第一个问题开始。）

四、阅读理解

1. What do true friends do when we make mistakes?

A. They leave us alone.
B. They laugh at us.
C. They support us and do not judge us.
D. They tell everyone about it.

2. How can we start making new friends?

A. By saying a simple hello and smiling.
B. By giving expensive gifts.
C. By staying quiet in class.
D. By moving to a new city.

3. What does keeping a friendship strong require?

A. A lot of money.
B. Living in the same city.
C. Real effort from both people.
D. Having the same hobby.

4. According to the article, friends help us feel ____.

A. more worried
B. less lonely and more confident
C. more tired
D. less interested in life

答案：1.C 2.A 3.C 4.B
