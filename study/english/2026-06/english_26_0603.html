存钱的简单方法

Nuouo2026/6/3大约 5 分钟英语学习每日英语基础英语

存钱的简单方法

一、英文原文

How to Save Money in Simple Ways

Saving money is an important skill for everyone. It helps you feel safe and prepared for the future. You do not need a lot of money to start saving today.

First, make a simple budget for your money each month. Write down how much money you earn from your job. Also write down how much you spend on food and transport.

A good way to save is the 50-30-20 rule. Spend 50% on things you need like food and rent. Put 20% of your money into a savings account.

You can also reduce your small daily costs. Make your coffee at home instead of buying it. Bring your own lunch to work or school every day.

Try not to buy things on impulse at the store. Wait 24 hours before you make a new purchase. Ask yourself if you really need the item.

Open a separate bank account for your savings. Put money into it right after you get your pay. Watch your savings grow slowly over time.

Saving money is not difficult when you start small. Every small amount counts over time. Your future self will thank you for saving.

（共 198 词）

你现在不需要很多钱就可以开始存钱。

三、语法简析

一般现在时（Simple Present Tense）

本文大量使用一般现在时，用于描述普遍事实和习惯性动作。

原句： Saving money is an important skill for everyone.

结构： 主语（Saving money）+ 系动词（is）+ 表语（an important skill）。一般现在时表示这是一个普遍真理。

练习造句：

  • Reading books helps us learn new things.（读书帮助我们学习新东西。）
  • Exercise makes our body strong.（运动使我们的身体强壮。）

动词不定式作目的状语（Infinitive of Purpose）

"to + 动词原形"结构用于说明做某事的目的。

原句： You do not need a lot of money to start saving today.

结构： You do not need a lot of money + to start saving today。这里 to start saving 表示"需要钱"的目的——开始存钱。

练习造句：

  • I go to the library to read books.（我去图书馆看书。）
  • She saves money to buy a new bike.（她存钱是为了买一辆新自行车。）

祈使句（Imperative Sentences）

祈使句以动词原形开头，用于给出指令或建议。

原句： Make your coffee at home instead of buying it.

结构： 动词原形（Make）+ 宾语（your coffee）+ 状语（at home）。主语 you 被省略。

练习造句：

  • Drink more water every day.（每天多喝水。）
  • Put your money in a safe place.（把钱放在安全的地方。）

四、阅读理解

1. What is the first step to save money according to the article?

A. Buy coffee at home
B. Make a simple budget
C. Open a bank account
D. Wait 24 hours

2. What does the 50-30-20 rule suggest you do with 20% of your money?

A. Spend it on fun things
B. Spend it on food and rent
C. Put it into a savings account
D. Use it for daily costs

3. What should you do before buying something on impulse?

A. Ask a friend for advice
B. Wait 24 hours
C. Check your budget
D. Open a new account

4. What does the article say about small amounts of money?

A. They are not useful
B. They count over time
C. They are difficult to save
D. They should be spent on fun

答案：1.B 2.C 3.B 4.B
