社交媒体与青少年心理健康：每天三小时的隐性代价

来源： The Guardian, Study links children's social media use with anxiety and depression in teenage years | 2026年3月22日
原文链接： https://www.theguardian.com/media/2026/mar/22/study-children-social-media-anxiety-depression
题材： 社会与心理健康 · 适合考研英语阅读

一、英文原文

Children who spend more than three hours a day on social media are significantly more likely to develop symptoms of anxiety and depression during their teenage years, according to a major new study conducted by researchers at Imperial College London. The analysis, which tracked the digital habits and mental health outcomes of thousands of young people over several years, found a clear correlation between prolonged social media engagement and deteriorating psychological well-being. Compared with peers who spent roughly 30 minutes online each day, heavy users exhibited markedly higher rates of emotional distress. Professor Mireille Toledano, the study's principal investigator, emphasized that the findings reveal "a clear trend in terms of the amount of time spent on social media and mental health outcomes." The research arrives at a pivotal moment, as the British government has launched a public consultation on potential measures to shield children online, including a partial social media ban for under-16s. Experts argue that as digital platforms, usage patterns, and online content continue to evolve rapidly, sustained research efforts are essential to fully understand how social media shapes the psychological landscape of an entire generation.

三、全文翻译

根据伦敦帝国理工学院研究人员开展的一项大规模新研究，每天在社交媒体上花费超过三小时的儿童，在青少年时期出现焦虑和抑郁症状的可能性显著更高。该分析追踪了数千名年轻人的数字使用习惯和心理健康状况，历时数年，发现长时间使用社交媒体与心理健康恶化之间存在明显的相关性。与每天上网约30分钟的同龄人相比，重度使用者表现出明显更高的情绪困扰比例。该研究的首席研究员 Mireille Toledano 教授强调，研究结果揭示了"社交媒体使用时长与心理健康结果之间存在明显的趋势关系"。此项研究发布于一个关键时刻——英国政府已就保护儿童上网的潜在措施展开公众征询，其中包括对16岁以下青少年实施部分社交媒体禁令。专家们指出，随着数字平台、使用模式和在线内容的持续快速演变，持续的研究工作对于全面理解社交媒体如何塑造一整代人的心理格局至关重要。

五、语法简析

句型一：who 引导的定语从句 + 比较级结构

Children who spend more than three hours a day on social media are significantly more likely to develop symptoms of anxiety and depression during their teenage years, according to a major new study conducted by researchers at Imperial College London.

结构分析：

  • who spend ... 是定语从句，修饰 children，说明是"哪类儿童"
  • be more likely to do sth. 是考研高频结构，表示"更有可能做某事"
  • significantly 修饰比较级 more likely，表示程度"显著地"
  • according to 引出信息来源；conducted by 是过去分词短语作后置定语修饰 study

考研重点： "be likely to" 及其比较级是考研英语完形填空和翻译中的常考点。注意区分 likely（形容词）和 possibly/probably（副词）的用法差异。

句型二：as 引导的时间/原因状语从句 + 主句倒装否定式

The research arrives at a pivotal moment, as the British government has launched a public consultation on potential measures to shield children online, including a partial social media ban for under-16s.

结构分析：

  • as 引导原因状语从句，解释"为什么说此时是关键时刻"
  • to shield children online 是不定式短语作目的状语，修饰 measures
  • including ... 是现在分词短语作补充说明，列举具体措施
  • arrive at a moment 是固定搭配，表示"在某一时刻到来/发布"

考研重点： including 作补充说明的用法在阅读理解中频繁出现，相当于"其中包括……"。shield ... from ... 是高级替换 protect 的写作词汇，值得积累。
