金钱与理财基础

Nuouo2026/5/25大约 5 分钟英语学习每日英语基础英语

金钱与理财基础

一、英文原文

Money Basics for Young People

Learning about money is an important skill. Many young people do not know how to save or spend wisely. Good money habits can help you in the future.

You can earn money by doing part-time jobs. Helping your neighbors with small tasks is also a good way. Every small amount of money is valuable.

A budget is a plan for your money. It helps you see how much you earn and spend. Writing down your expenses is a good habit.

Saving money means setting aside part of your earnings. Even saving a little each week adds up over time. A savings account keeps your money safe.

It is important to distinguish between needs and wants. Food and shelter are necessities. Video games and fancy clothes are wants.

A credit card lets you buy things now and pay later. But you must pay back the money on time. If you do not, you will owe extra interest.

Compound interest makes your money grow faster. The longer you save, the more money you will have. Start saving early to enjoy this benefit.

（共 180 词）

二、逐句精读

第一段

Learning about money is an important skill.

学习关于金钱的知识是一项重要的技能。

Many young people do not know how to save or spend wisely.

很多年轻人不知道如何明智地储蓄或消费。

Good money habits can help you in the future.

良好的金钱习惯可以在未来帮助你。

单词词性释义
skilln.技能
wiselyadv.明智地

第二段

You can earn money by doing part-time jobs.

你可以通过做兼职工作来赚钱。

Helping your neighbors with small tasks is also a good way.

帮助邻居完成小任务也是一个好方法。

Every small amount of money is valuable.

每一小笔钱都是有价值的。

单词词性释义
earnv.赚取
part-timeadj.兼职的
valuableadj.有价值的

第三段

A budget is a plan for your money.

预算是你金钱的计划。

It helps you see how much you earn and spend.

它帮助你了解你赚了多少、花了多少。

Writing down your expenses is a good habit.

写下你的开销是一个好习惯。

单词词性释义
budgetn.预算
expensen.开销、费用

第四段

Saving money means setting aside part of your earnings.

储蓄意味着留出一部分你的收入。

Even saving a little each week adds up over time.

即使每周只存一点，时间长了也会积少成多。

A savings account keeps your money safe.

储蓄账户可以让你的钱保持安全。

单词词性释义
savingsn.储蓄
accountn.账户
set aside短语留出、拨出
add up短语积累、积少成多

第五段

It is important to distinguish between needs and wants.

区分需求和想要是很重要的。

Food and shelter are necessities.

食物和住所是生活必需品。

Video games and fancy clothes are wants.

电子游戏和花哨的衣服是想要的东西。

单词词性释义
distinguishv.区分、辨别
necessityn.必需品
fancyadj.花哨的、高档的

第六段

A credit card lets you buy things now and pay later.

信用卡让你现在买东西、以后付款。

But you must pay back the money on time.

但你必须按时还钱。

If you do not, you will owe extra interest.

如果你不还，你将欠下额外的利息。

单词词性释义
creditn.信用
owev.欠（钱）
interestn.利息

第七段

Compound interest makes your money grow faster.

复利让你的钱增长得更快。

The longer you save, the more money you will have.

你存的时间越长，你拥有的钱就越多。

Start saving early to enjoy this benefit.

尽早开始储蓄，享受这个好处。

单词词性释义
compoundadj.复合的
benefitn.好处、利益

三、语法简析

"by doing" 表示方式

"by + 动词-ing" 表示"通过做某事"的方式。

原句： You can earn money by doing part-time jobs.

结构： 主语 + can + 动词 + by + doing...

练习造句：

  • I learn English by reading books every day.（我通过每天读书来学习英语。）
  • She improved her health by exercising regularly.（她通过定期锻炼改善了健康。）

"the more...the more..." 比较句型

"the + 比较级..., the + 比较级..." 表示"越……就越……"。

原句： The longer you save, the more money you will have.

结构： The + 比较级 + 主语 + 动词, the + 比较级 + 主语 + 动词...

练习造句：

  • The more you practice, the better you will become.（你练习得越多，就会变得越好。）
  • The harder you work, the more you will achieve.（你越努力，收获就越多。）

"if" 条件句（第一类条件句）

"if + 一般现在时, will + 动词原形" 表示将来可能发生的条件。

原句： If you do not, you will owe extra interest.

结构： If + 条件（现在时）, 结果（will + 动词原形）

练习造句：

  • If it rains tomorrow, I will stay at home.（如果明天下雨，我就待在家里。）
  • If you save more, you will have more money.（如果你多存点，你就会有更多的钱。）

四、阅读理解

1. What is a budget?

A. A type of bank account
B. A plan for your money
C. A kind of credit card
D. A way to earn money

2. Why should you pay back credit card money on time?

A. To get more credit
B. To avoid paying extra interest
C. To save more money
D. To earn compound interest

3. What does "adds up" mean in the article?

A. Adds numbers together
B. Grows or increases over time
C. Adds more money to your account
D. Adds up the total expenses

4. Which of the following is a "want" according to the article?

A. Food
B. Shelter
C. Fancy clothes
D. A savings account

答案：1.B 2.B 3.B 4.C
