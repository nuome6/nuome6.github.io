地理与天气

Nuouo2026/5/27大约 6 分钟英语学习每日英语基础英语

地理与天气

一、英文原文

Geography and Weather

Geography helps us understand the world around us. The Earth has many different landforms and climates. Mountains, rivers, and oceans are all part of geography.

Weather changes every day and affects our daily lives. Sunny days make people feel happy and energetic. Rainy days can make some people feel tired.

Snow falls in cold regions during winter. The climate in tropical areas is warm all year round. Deserts are very dry and hot during the day. At night, deserts can become surprisingly cold.

Forests receive a lot of rain and have many trees. The weather forecast tells us what to expect tomorrow. We can check the forecast on our phones or TV.

An umbrella is useful on rainy days. Wearing warm clothes is important in cold weather. The seasons change because of the Earth's tilt.

Spring brings flowers and warmer temperatures. Summer is the hottest season in most places. Autumn leaves turn red, orange, and yellow. Winter brings snow and shorter days.

Natural disasters like floods and storms can be dangerous. Scientists study the weather to help keep people safe. Wind is caused by moving air in the atmosphere. Learning about geography helps us appreciate our planet. The weather connects us all, no matter where we live.

（共 210 词）

二、逐句精读

第一段

地理帮助我们了解周围的世界。

The Earth has many different landforms and climates.

地球有许多不同的地形和气候。

Mountains, rivers, and oceans are all part of geography.

山脉、河流和海洋都是地理的一部分。

geographyn.地理

第二段

天气每天都在变化，影响着我们的日常生活。

晴天让人感到快乐和充满活力。

雨天会让一些人感到疲倦。

热带地区的气候全年温暖。

沙漠白天非常干燥和炎热。

单词词性释义

三、语法简析

1. make + 宾语 + 形容词（使役动词结构）

make 在这里表示"使……变得"，后接宾语和形容词补语，描述某种事物对人的影响。

原句： Sunny days make people feel happy and energetic. / Rainy days can make some people feel tired.

结构： 主语 + make + 宾语 + 形容词/动词原形

练习造句：

  • The good news made her smile.（这个好消息让她笑了。）
  • Hot weather makes me want to drink cold water.（热天让我想喝冷水。）

2. because of + 名词短语（表原因）

because of 后接名词或名词短语，表示原因。注意与 because（后接句子）的区别。

原句： The seasons change because of the Earth's tilt.

结构： 句子 + because of + 名词短语

练习造句：

  • We stayed home because of the heavy rain.（因为大雨我们待在家里。）
  • She was late because of the traffic jam.（因为堵车她迟到了。）

3. no matter + 疑问词（无论……）

no matter where/what/when/how 等表示"无论……"，引导让步状语，强调不管条件如何，结果不变。

原句： The weather connects us all, no matter where we live.

结构： no matter + where/what/when/how + 句子

练习造句：

  • No matter what happens, I will support you.（无论发生什么，我都会支持你。）
  • No matter how hard it is, we should try.（无论多难，我们都应该尝试。）

四、阅读理解

1. What makes people feel happy and energetic according to the article?

A. Rainy days
B. Sunny days
C. Snowy days
D. Windy days

2. Why are deserts surprising at night?

A. They become very wet
B. They become surprisingly cold
C. They become very windy
D. They become tropical

3. What causes the seasons to change?

A. The distance from the sun
B. The amount of rain
C. The Earth's tilt
D. The wind direction

4. What is wind caused by?

A. The Earth's tilt
B. Moving air in the atmosphere
C. Mountains and rivers
D. The weather forecast

答案：1.B 2.B 3.C 4.B
