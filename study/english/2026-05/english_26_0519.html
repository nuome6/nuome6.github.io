心理健康与情绪管理

Nuouo2026/5/19大约 7 分钟英语学习每日英语基础英语

心理健康与情绪管理

一、英文原文

Managing Your Emotions for Better Mental Health

Everyone has good days and bad days. Sometimes we feel anxious or stressed without knowing why. These feelings are normal, but we need to learn how to manage them. Mental health is just as important as physical health.

When we feel sad or angry, it helps to pause and take a deep breath. This simple act can calm our mind and body. It gives us time to think before we react. Many people forget this and say things they later regret.

Writing down our feelings is another helpful method. A journal can help us understand what makes us happy or upset. We do not need to write a lot — just a few sentences each day can make a big difference.

Talking to someone we trust is also important. A friend, a teacher, or a family member can listen and offer support. We should never feel embarrassed to ask for help. Sharing our problems makes them feel smaller.

Exercise is another great way to improve our mood. A short walk or a few minutes of stretching can help us feel more relaxed. Our body and mind are connected, so moving our body helps our mind too.

It is also important to get enough sleep. When we are tired, we are more likely to feel irritable. A good night's sleep helps us think clearly and stay positive.

In conclusion, taking care of our mental health is not difficult. Small actions every day can help us feel better and live a happier life.

（共 210 词）

二、逐句精读

第一段

Everyone has good days and bad days.

每个人都有好日子和坏日子。

Sometimes we feel anxious or stressed without knowing why.

有时候我们会感到焦虑或有压力，却不知道为什么。

These feelings are normal, but we need to learn how to manage them.

这些感受是正常的，但我们需要学习如何管理它们。

Mental health is just as important as physical health.

心理健康和身体健康一样重要。

单词词性释义
anxiousadj.焦虑的
stressedadj.有压力的
normaladj.正常的
managev.管理、应对
mentaladj.心理的、精神的

第二段

When we feel sad or angry, it helps to pause and take a deep breath.

当我们感到悲伤或生气时，暂停一下深呼吸会有帮助。

This simple act can calm our mind and body.

这个简单的动作可以让我们身心平静。

It gives us time to think before we react.

它让我们在做出反应之前有时间思考。

Many people forget this and say things they later regret.

很多人忘记这一点，说了一些后来后悔的话。

单词词性释义
pausev.暂停
calmv.使平静
reactv.反应
regretv.后悔

第三段

Writing down our feelings is another helpful method.

写下我们的感受是另一种有用的方法。

A journal can help us understand what makes us happy or upset.

写日记可以帮助我们了解什么让我们开心或难过。

We do not need to write a lot — just a few sentences each day can make a big difference.

我们不需要写很多——每天写几句话就能带来很大的改变。

单词词性释义
writingn./v.写作
methodn.方法
sentencesn.句子

第四段

Talking to someone we trust is also important.

和我们信任的人交谈也很重要。

A friend, a teacher, or a family member can listen and offer support.

朋友、老师或家人可以倾听并给予支持。

We should never feel embarrassed to ask for help.

我们不应该因为寻求帮助而感到尴尬。

Sharing our problems makes them feel smaller.

分享我们的问题会让它们看起来没那么严重。

单词词性释义

supportn.支持
embarrassedadj.尴尬的

第五段

运动是改善情绪的另一种好方法。

散一会儿步或做几分钟拉伸可以帮助我们感到更放松。

我们的身体和心灵是相连的，所以活动身体也有助于我们的大脑。

单词词性释义

第六段

当我们疲倦时，更容易感到烦躁。

好好睡一觉有助于我们思路清晰并保持积极。

单词词性释义

第七段

总之，照顾我们的心理健康并不难。

每天的小行动可以帮助我们感觉更好，过上更幸福的生活。

单词词性释义

三、语法简析

现在分词作主语

第三段开头用 "Writing down our feelings" 作为主语。动词加 -ing 变成名词性短语，可以放在句首当主语。

原句： Writing down our feelings is another helpful method.

结构： Writing (现在分词短语) + is (系动词) + another helpful method (表语)

练习造句：

  • Reading books every day is a good habit.（每天读书是一个好习惯。）
  • Drinking enough water helps your body stay healthy.（喝足够的水有助于身体健康。）

"It is + adj. + to do" 句型

第六段用 "It is also important to get enough sleep"。这里的 it 是形式主语，真正主语是后面的不定式 to get enough sleep。

原句： It is also important to get enough sleep.

结构： It (形式主语) + is (系动词) + important (形容词) + to get enough sleep (真正主语)

练习造句：

  • It is easy to learn English if you practice every day.（如果你每天练习，学英语很容易。）
  • It is necessary to listen to your teacher carefully.（认真听老师讲课是必要的。）

"so" 连接因果关系

第五段用 "Our body and mind are connected, so moving our body helps our mind too."。so 表示因果关系，前半句是原因，后半句是结果。

原句： Our body and mind are connected, so moving our body helps our mind too.

结构： 原因句 + , so + 结果句

练习造句：

  • I studied hard, so I passed the exam.（我努力学习了，所以我通过了考试。）
  • It was raining, so we stayed at home.（下雨了，所以我们待在家里。）

四、阅读理解

1. According to the passage, what should we do when we feel angry?

A. Ignore the feeling
B. Pause and take a deep breath
C. Talk to a stranger
D. Go to sleep immediately

2. What can a journal help us do?

A. Write a novel
B. Learn new words
C. Understand what makes us happy or upset
D. Remember our homework

3. Why is exercise good for mental health?

A. It makes us stronger
B. It helps us meet new friends
C. Our body and mind are connected
D. It costs no money

4. What is the main idea of this passage?

A. Physical health is more important than mental health
B. We should exercise every day
C. Small daily actions can help improve our mental health
D. Only doctors can help with mental health problems

答案：1.B 2.C 3.C 4.C
