运动与锻炼的好处

Nuouo2026/5/26大约 8 分钟英语学习每日英语基础英语

运动与锻炼的好处

一、英文原文

The Benefits of Regular Exercise

Exercise is very important for our health. Many doctors say we should move our bodies every day. Even a short walk can make a big difference. When we exercise, our muscles get stronger and our bones become harder. Good exercise habits help us stay healthy for a long time.

Walking is one of the easiest forms of exercise. You do not need any special equipment. Just put on your shoes and go outside. Walking for thirty minutes each day can improve your heart health. It also helps you sleep better at night.

Running is another great choice for exercise. It makes your heart beat faster and your breathing become deeper. Running can help you lose weight and feel more energetic. Many people enjoy running in the morning because it gives them a good start to the day.

Swimming is good exercise that is easy on your joints. The water supports your body, so there is less pressure on your knees and ankles. Swimming works many muscles at the same time. It is a good choice for people of all ages.

Playing sports is a fun way to exercise. You can play basketball, soccer, or tennis with your friends. When you play sports, you do not feel like you are exercising. You just feel like you are having fun. Sports also teach you about teamwork and fairness.

Exercise is good for your mind, too. When you move your body, your brain makes special chemicals. These chemicals make you feel happy and relaxed. Many people say they feel less stressed after exercise. A healthy body and a healthy mind go together.

If you do not exercise much, start with small steps. Take a ten-minute walk today. Try to do a few simple stretches. Gradually do more exercise each week. Your body will thank you, and you will feel much better.

（共 309 词）

三、语法简析

现在时态的一般规律

英语中描述普遍事实和习惯时，使用一般现在时。主语为第三人称单数（he/she/it）时，动词加 -s 或 -es。

原句： Walking is one of the easiest forms of exercise.

结构： 主语 (Walking) + 系动词 (is) + 表语 (one of the easiest forms)

练习造句：

  • Swimming is a great way to stay fit.（游泳是保持健康的好方法。）
  • Reading helps you learn new things.（阅读帮助你学习新知识。）

比较级的简单用法

形容词加 -er 或在前面加 more 构成比较级，用于比较两者的差异。常见搭配：more + 形容词、形容词 + er + than。

原句： It makes your heart beat faster and your breathing become deeper.

结构： make + 宾语 + 动词原形 + 比较级形容词

练习造句：

  • She runs faster than her brother.（她跑得比她哥哥快。）
  • This book is more interesting than that one.（这本书比那本更有趣。）

"one of the + 最高级 + 名词复数" 结构

这个结构表示"最……之一"，后面接名词复数形式，谓语动词用单数。是中考和高考的常见考点。

原句： Walking is one of the easiest forms of exercise.

结构： 主语 + is + one of the + 最高级 + 名词复数

练习造句：

  • Beijing is one of the largest cities in the world.（北京是世界上最大的城市之一。）
  • She is one of the best students in our class.（她是我们班最好的学生之一。）

四、阅读理解

1. What do many doctors suggest about exercise?

A. We should exercise only on weekends.
B. We should move our bodies every day.
C. We should only run for exercise.
D. We should not walk for exercise.

2. Why is swimming good for older people?

A. It is the cheapest exercise.
B. It makes you lose weight fast.
C. It is easy on the joints.
D. It teaches teamwork.

3. What happens in your brain when you exercise?

A. Your brain makes special chemicals.
B. Your brain stops working.
C. Your brain gets smaller.
D. Your brain becomes tired.

4. What should beginners do to start exercising?

A. Run for one hour every day.
B. Join a sports team immediately.
C. Start with small steps like a ten-minute walk.
D. Only do swimming exercises.

答案：1.B 2.C 3.A 4.C
