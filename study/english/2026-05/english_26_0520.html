一、英文原文

Eating Well for a Healthy Life

Food is the fuel that keeps our bodies running every day.
A balanced diet includes vegetables, fruits, grains, and protein.
Eating too much sugar can cause health problems like diabetes.
Drinking enough water is just as important as eating well.
Many people skip breakfast, but it gives energy for the morning.
Fresh fruits are a great snack because they are full of vitamins.
Cooking at home is healthier than eating fast food every day.
Vegetables have fiber that helps our digestive system work well.
Fish and eggs are good sources of protein for growing children.
Eating colorful food often means you are getting many nutrients.
Reading food labels helps you know what you are really eating.
Some people eat too much salt without even knowing it.
Nuts and seeds are small but contain healthy fats and vitamins.
When we feel tired, a good meal can help us feel better quickly.
Parents should teach children to try new foods from a young age.
Too much fast food can make people gain weight very easily.
A healthy breakfast might include oatmeal, milk, and an apple.
Our bodies need different foods to stay strong and prevent illness.
Walking after a meal helps your body digest food more easily.
People who eat well often sleep better at night too.
School lunches should offer balanced choices for every student.
Seasonal fruits are usually fresher and cost less money.
Good eating habits start when we are young and last a lifetime.

三、语法简析

比较结构 "just as...as"

表示两者同等重要或程度相同。

原句： Drinking enough water is just as important as eating well.

结构： A + is + just as + adj. + as + B（A和B同样……）

练习造句：

  • Reading is just as useful as writing for learning English.（阅读和写作对学英语同样有用。）
  • Sleeping early is just as important as exercising.（早睡和锻炼同样重要。）

并列连词 "but" 表转折

连接两个意思相反的分句，表示转折关系。

原句： Many people skip breakfast, but it gives energy for the morning.

结构： 逗号 + but + 转折分句（前句说现象，后句说相反的结论）

练习造句：

  • The test was difficult, but I passed it.（考试很难，但我通过了。）
  • She is busy, but she always helps others.（她很忙，但她总是帮助别人。）

介词短语 "without even knowing"

without + doing 表示"没有做某事"，加 even 强调"甚至没有意识到"。

原句： Some people eat too much salt without even knowing it.

结构： 主语 + 动词 + 宾语 + without + doing（在没有做……的情况下）

练习造句：

  • He finished the work without asking for help.（他没有请求帮助就完成了工作。）
  • She left without saying goodbye.（她没说再见就走了。）
