艺术与创造力的力量

Nuouo2026/5/23大约 6 分钟英语学习每日英语基础英语

艺术与创造力的力量

一、英文原文

The Power of Art and Creativity

Art is everywhere in our daily lives. We see it on walls and in museums. Music is a form of art too. Art makes our world more colorful.

Creativity helps us solve many problems. When we are creative, we think in new ways. Children are naturally creative and curious. They use imagination to play and learn. We should keep being creative.

Drawing is a simple way to make art. You only need a pencil and paper. Painting uses colors to show our feelings. Red can mean love or anger.

Playing an instrument takes time and practice. The piano is a popular choice. Singing is the simplest form of music. We can sing alone or with friends. Music can make us feel happy.

Writing is another kind of art. Anyone can write a short story. Stories can be about real life. They can also come from imagination. Writing helps us express our thoughts.

Art brings people together in many ways. Museums show great works of famous artists. We can make art with our own hands. Crafts like pottery are fun for all.

You do not need to be an artist. Just try something new and have fun. Draw a picture or write a poem. Creativity is a special gift we all have.

（共 211 词）

二、逐句精读

第一段

Art is everywhere in our daily lives.

艺术在我们日常生活中无处不在。

We see it on walls and in museums.

我们在墙壁上和博物馆里都能看到它。

Music is a form of art too.

音乐也是一种艺术形式。

Art makes our world more colorful.

艺术让我们的世界更加丰富多彩。

单词词性释义
museumn.博物馆
formn.形式
colorfuladj.多彩的

第二段

Creativity helps us solve many problems.

创造力帮助我们解决许多问题。

When we are creative, we think in new ways.

当我们有创造力时，我们会用新的方式思考。

Children are naturally creative and curious.

孩子们天生就有创造力和好奇心。

They use imagination to play and learn.

他们用想象力来玩耍和学习。

We should keep being creative.

我们应该保持创造力。

单词词性释义
creativityn.创造力
creativeadj.有创造力的
curiousadj.好奇的
imaginationn.想象力

第三段

Drawing is a simple way to make art.

画画是一种简单的艺术方式。

You only need a pencil and paper.

你只需要一支铅笔和纸。

Painting uses colors to show our feelings.

绘画用颜色来表达我们的感受。

Red can mean love or anger.

红色可以代表爱或愤怒。

单词词性释义
drawingn.画，绘画
paintingn.绘画，油画
feelingsn.感受，感情

第四段

Playing an instrument takes time and practice.

演奏乐器需要时间和练习。

The piano is a popular choice.

钢琴是一个受欢迎的选择。

Singing is the simplest form of music.

唱歌是最简单的音乐形式。

We can sing alone or with friends.

我们可以独自唱或和朋友一起唱。

Music can make us feel happy.

音乐能让我们感到快乐。

单词词性释义
instrumentn.乐器
practicen.练习
popularadj.受欢迎的

第五段

Writing is another kind of art.

写作是另一种艺术形式。

Anyone can write a short story.

任何人都可以写一个短篇故事。

Stories can be about real life.

故事可以是关于真实生活的。

They can also come from imagination.

它们也可以来自想象力。

Writing helps us express our thoughts.

写作帮助我们表达自己的想法。

单词词性释义
expressv.表达
thoughtsn.想法，思想

第六段

Art brings people together in many ways.

艺术以多种方式把人们聚集在一起。

Museums show great works of famous artists.

博物馆展示著名艺术家的伟大作品。

We can make art with our own hands.

我们可以用自己的双手创造艺术。

Crafts like pottery are fun for all.

陶艺等手工艺对所有人都很有趣。

单词词性释义
craftsn.手工艺
potteryn.陶艺
famousadj.著名的

第七段

You do not need to be an artist.

你不需要成为一个艺术家。

Just try something new and have fun.

只需尝试新事物，享受乐趣。

Draw a picture or write a poem.

画一幅画或写一首诗。

Creativity is a special gift we all have.

创造力是我们每个人都拥有的特殊天赋。

单词词性释义
artistn.艺术家
giftn.天赋，礼物

三、语法简析

make + 宾语 + 形容词

"make + 宾语 + 形容词"表示"使……变得……"，是常见的使役结构。

原句： Art makes our world more colorful.

结构： make（使）+ our world（宾语）+ more colorful（宾语补足语）

练习造句：

  • This song makes me happy.（这首歌让我很开心。）
  • The good news makes us excited.（好消息让我们很兴奋。）

need to do 表示"需要做某事"

"need to + 动词原形"表示"需要做某事"，用于表达必要性。

原句： You do not need to be an artist.

结构： need（需要）+ to be（成为，不定式）+ an artist（名词短语）

练习造句：

  • I need to study harder.（我需要更努力学习。）
  • She needs to practice more.（她需要多练习。）

can + 动词原形表示能力或可能性

"can + 动词原形"既可以表示能力（"能够"），也可以表示可能性（"可以"）。

原句： We can sing alone or with friends.

结构： can（能）+ sing（动词原形）+ alone or with friends（方式状语）

练习造句：

  • I can speak English and Chinese.（我会说英语和中文。）
  • You can learn to draw at home.（你可以在家学画画。）

四、阅读理解

1. What does art do to our world according to the article?

A. It makes it quieter
B. It makes it more colorful
C. It makes it more expensive
D. It makes it simpler

2. Why are children naturally good at creativity?

A. They go to art school
B. They read many books
C. They use imagination to play and learn
D. They have more free time

3. What does the article say about singing?

A. It is the hardest form of music
B. It needs expensive equipment
C. It is the simplest form of music
D. It can only be done alone

4. What is the main message of the last paragraph?

A. Only artists can make art
B. Art is too difficult for most people
C. Everyone has the gift of creativity
D. You must draw to be creative

答案：1.B 2.C 3.C 4.C
