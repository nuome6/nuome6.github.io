每天走路的好处

Nuouo2026/5/10大约 6 分钟英语学习每日英语基础英语

每天走路的好处

一、英文原文

Benefits of Walking Every Day

Walking is one of the best things you can do for your health. It is free, easy, and anyone can do it. You do not need special clothes or a gym. All you need is a pair of good shoes.

Walking helps you keep a healthy weight. When you walk, your body burns calories. The more you walk, the more calories you burn. Walking for 30 minutes a day can help you lose weight over time.

Walking is also good for your heart. It makes your heart stronger and helps keep your blood pressure low. Studies show that people who walk every day have a lower risk of heart disease. Just 15 minutes of fast walking each day can reduce your risk of early death by about 20 percent.

Walking can improve your mood. When you walk outside, you feel less stressed and more relaxed. Walking with friends or family can also make you happier.

Walking helps your brain work better. Studies say that people who walk regularly can think more clearly. Walking may also help you sleep better at night.

The CDC says adults should walk at least 150 minutes per week. That is about 30 minutes a day, five days a week. You can walk to work, walk your dog, or just walk around your neighborhood.

Start today. Even a short walk is better than no walk at all.

（改编自 Healthline、BBC Science Focus、CDC）

二、逐句精读

第一段

散步是你能为健康做的最好的事情之一。

It is free, easy, and anyone can do it.

它是免费的、简单的，任何人都可以做。

You do not need special clothes or a gym.

你不需要特别的衣服或健身房。

All you need is a pair of good shoes.

你需要的只是一双好鞋。

单词词性释义
specialadj.特别的
gymn.健身房
a pair of短语一双/一副

第二段

Walking helps you keep a healthy weight.

散步帮助你保持健康的体重。

When you walk, your body burns calories.

当你走路时，你的身体会燃烧卡路里。

The more you walk, the more calories you burn.

你走得越多，燃烧的卡路里就越多。

Walking for 30 minutes a day can help you lose weight over time.

每天走30分钟，日积月累可以帮助你减肥。

单词词性释义
weightn.体重
caloriesn.卡路里
burnv.燃烧
over time短语随着时间推移

第三段

Walking is also good for your heart.

散步对你的心脏也有好处。

It makes your heart stronger and helps keep your blood pressure low.

它让你的心脏更强壮，帮助保持低血压。

Studies show that people who walk every day have a lower risk of heart disease.

研究表明，每天走路的人患心脏病的风险更低。

Just 15 minutes of fast walking each day can reduce your risk of early death by about 20 percent.

每天只需快走15分钟，就能将早死风险降低约20%。

单词词性释义
blood pressure短语血压
riskn.风险

散步可以改善你的情绪。

当你在户外走路时，你会感到压力更小、更放松。

和朋友或家人一起散步也能让你更快乐。

那大约是每天30分钟，每周5天。

第七段

今天就开始吧。

即使短距离散步也比完全不走好。

三、语法简析

The more... the more... （越……越……）

这是一个非常实用的句型，表示两个事物成正比变化。

原句： The more you walk, the more calories you burn.

你走得越多，燃烧的卡路里就越多。

结构： The + 比较级, the + 比较级

练习造句：

  • The more you read, the more words you know.（你读得越多，认识的单词就越多。）
  • The more you practice, the better you become.（你练习得越多，就变得越好。）

one of the best things you can do（你能做的最好的事情之一）

这是一个常见的高级表达，用来强调某事的重要性。

原句： Walking is one of the best things you can do for your health.

结构： one of the + 最高级 + 名词 + (that/which) + 从句

练习造句：

  • Reading is one of the best things you can do for your brain.（阅读是你能为大脑做的最好的事情之一。）
  • Sleep is one of the most important things you need every day.（睡眠是你每天都需要的最重要的事情之一。）

It is + adj. + to do（做某事是……的）

这是一个基础但非常常用的句型。

原句： It is free, easy, and anyone can do it.

结构： It is + 形容词 + (for sb.) + to do sth.

练习造句：

  • It is important to drink water every day.（每天喝水很重要。）
  • It is easy to start walking.（开始走路很容易。）

四、阅读理解

1. According to the article, what do you need to start walking?

A. A gym membership
B. Special clothes
C. A pair of good shoes
D. A running coach

2. How many minutes of walking per week does the CDC recommend for adults?

A. 30 minutes
B. 75 minutes
C. 120 minutes
D. 150 minutes

3. What can walking do for your brain?

A. Make it bigger
B. Help you think more clearly
C. Give you better eyesight
D. Make you memorize faster

4. What is the main idea of this article?

A. Running is better than walking
B. Walking has many health benefits and is easy to start
C. You should walk at least one hour every day
D. Only young people should walk

答案：1.C 2.D 3.B 4.B
