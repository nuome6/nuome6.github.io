身体发育与健康成长

Nuouo2026/5/28大约 5 分钟英语学习每日英语基础英语

身体发育与健康成长

一、英文原文

Growing Up Healthy

Our bodies grow and change every day. Young people need the right food to grow well. A balanced diet helps the body get nutrition.

Fruits and vegetables are full of vitamins. They help the body work in a healthy way. We should eat many different colors of food.

Protein helps build strong muscles. Meat, eggs, and beans are good sources. Calcium makes our bones hard and strong.

Exercise is also very important for growth. It keeps our body strong and our mind happy. We can run, jump, stretch, or play sports.

Sleep helps the body repair and grow. Young people need eight to ten hours every night. The body does important work during rest.

Water is essential for every body function. It helps move nutrients to all parts. We should drink six to eight glasses daily.

Growing up healthy also means taking care of our emotions. Spending time with family makes us feel loved. Let us start caring for our health today.

（共 203 词）

二、逐句精读

第一段

Our bodies grow and change every day.

我们的身体每天都在生长和变化。

Young people need the right food to grow well.

年轻人需要正确的食物才能好好成长。

A balanced diet helps the body get nutrition.

均衡的饮食能帮助身体获得营养。

单词词性释义
foodn.食物
balancedadj.均衡的
nutritionn.营养

第二段

Fruits and vegetables are full of vitamins.

水果和蔬菜富含维生素。

They help the body work in a healthy way.

它们帮助身体以健康的方式运作。

We should eat many different colors of food.

我们应该吃很多不同颜色的食物。

单词词性释义
fruitn.水果
vegetablen.蔬菜
vitaminn.维生素

第三段

Protein helps build strong muscles.

蛋白质帮助强健肌肉。

Meat, eggs, and beans are good sources.

肉、蛋和豆类是很好的来源。

Calcium makes our bones hard and strong.

钙让我们的骨骼坚硬强壮。

单词词性释义
proteinn.蛋白质
musclen.肌肉
calciumn.钙
bonen.骨骼

第四段

Exercise is also very important for growth.

运动对成长也很重要。

It keeps our body strong and our mind happy.

它让我们的身体强壮，心情愉快。

We can run, jump, stretch, or play sports.

我们可以跑步、跳跃、拉伸或做运动。

单词词性释义
exercisen.运动
stretchv.伸展

第五段

Sleep helps the body repair and grow.

睡眠帮助身体修复和成长。

Young people need eight to ten hours every night.

年轻人每晚需要八到十小时的睡眠。

The body does important work during rest.

身体在休息时做着重要的工作。

单词词性释义
repairv.修复
restn./v.休息

第六段

Water is essential for every body function.

水对身体的每一项功能都至关重要。

It helps move nutrients to all parts.

它帮助将营养输送到全身。

We should drink six to eight glasses daily.

我们每天应该喝六到八杯水。

单词词性释义
essentialadj.至关重要的
functionn.功能
nutrientn.营养物质
glassn.杯

第七段

Growing up healthy also means taking care of our emotions.

健康成长也意味着关注我们的情绪。

Spending time with family makes us feel loved.

和家人在一起让我们感受到爱。

Let us start caring for our health today.

让我们从今天开始关心自己的健康吧。

单词词性释义
emotionn.情感
carev.关心

三、语法简析

1. 动名词作主语

动名词（V-ing 形式）可以放在句首作主语，表示一个动作或行为。

原句： Growing up healthy also means taking care of our emotions.

结构： 动名词短语 + 动词 + 宾语

练习造句：

  • Swimming is good for your health.（游泳对你的健康有好处。）
  • Reading books helps you learn new things.（读书帮助你学到新东西。）

2. make + 宾语 + 形容词（使役动词）

make 后面接宾语再接形容词，表示"使……变得……"。

原句： Calcium makes our bones hard and strong.

结构： make + 宾语 + 形容词

练习造句：

  • The good news makes her happy.（这个好消息让她开心。）
  • Exercise makes our body healthy.（运动使我们的身体健康。）

3. should 表示建议

should 是情态动词，表示"应该"，用来提出建议或忠告，后面接动词原形。

原句： We should drink six to eight glasses daily.

结构： 主语 + should + 动词原形

练习造句：

  • You should sleep early tonight.（你今晚应该早点睡。）
  • Students should read every day.（学生应该每天阅读。）

四、阅读理解

1. What helps the body get nutrition according to the article?

A. Exercise
B. Sleep
C. A balanced diet
D. Drinking water

2. How many hours of sleep do young people need every night?

A. Six to seven hours
B. Seven to eight hours
C. Eight to ten hours
D. Ten to twelve hours

3. What does calcium do for our body?

A. It builds muscles.
B. It makes bones hard and strong.
C. It helps us sleep well.
D. It moves nutrients to all parts.

4. What does "healthy" mean according to the last paragraph?

A. Eating only fruits and vegetables
B. Never getting sick
C. Feeling good in body and emotions
D. Exercising every day

答案：1.C 2.C 3.B 4.C
