时间管理的简单技巧

Nuouo2026/5/15大约 7 分钟英语学习每日英语基础英语

时间管理的简单技巧

一、英文原文

Simple Tips for Time Management

Time is very important in our daily lives. Many people feel that there are not enough hours in a day. They rush from morning to night but still have much to do. Good time management can help us feel calm and productive. It is a skill that anyone can learn with a little practice.

The first step is to make a plan for each day. Write down the tasks you need to complete. Put the most urgent tasks at the top of your list. This helps you know what to do first. Without a plan, you may waste time on things that are not important.

It is also helpful to set a specific time for each task. For example, you can study English from seven to eight in the morning. When the time is up, move to the next task. This keeps you on track and prevents you from spending too long on one thing.

Taking short breaks is very beneficial. After working for about forty minutes, rest for five minutes. You can stretch your body or drink some water. Short breaks help your brain stay fresh and focused.

Many students find it hard to stop looking at their phones. Phones are a big distraction for most people. Try putting your phone in another room when you need to concentrate. This small change can save you a lot of time.

Another useful tip is to do the hardest task first. This is sometimes called "eating the frog." When you finish the difficult work early, the rest of the day feels easy. You will also feel proud of yourself for completing it.

Finally, try to review your day before you go to bed. Think about what you did well and what you can do better tomorrow. Over time, these small habits will make a big difference in your life. You will have more time for the things you enjoy.

三、语法简析

不定式作表语（It is + adj. + to do）

这个句型用 "It is + 形容词 + to do something" 来表达做某事的感受或评价。

原句： It is also helpful to set a specific time for each task.

结构： It (形式主语) + is + helpful (形容词) + to set (不定式作真正主语)

练习造句：

  • It is important to eat breakfast every day.（每天吃早餐很重要。）
  • It is easy to learn English if you practice often.（如果你经常练习，学英语很容易。）

动名词作主语（Doing sth. is ...）

用动名词（动词 + ing）作为句子的主语，表示做某件事这个行为。

原句： Taking short breaks is very beneficial.

结构： Taking short breaks (动名词短语作主语) + is (系动词) + beneficial (表语)

练习造句：

  • Reading books every day is good for your mind.（每天读书对你的大脑有好处。）
  • Sleeping eight hours is necessary for students.（睡八小时对学生来说是必要的。）

"find it + adj. + to do" 句型

find 后面接 it 作形式宾语，再接形容词和不定式，表示觉得做某事怎么样。

原句： Many students find it hard to stop looking at their phones.

结构： Many students (主语) + find (谓语) + it (形式宾语) + hard (宾补) + to stop looking at... (真正宾语)

练习造句：

  • I find it difficult to wake up early in winter.（我觉得冬天早起很难。）
  • She finds it easy to talk to her friends.（她觉得和朋友聊天很容易。）
