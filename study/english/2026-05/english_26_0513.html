阅读与写作习惯

一、英文原文

Reading and Writing Habits

Reading is one of the best ways to learn new things every day. You can read books, articles, or even short stories. Many people like to read before they go to sleep. Reading helps your brain stay active and strong. It also opens your mind to new ideas and different cultures. When you read often, your vocabulary grows naturally over time.

Writing is also a very important skill for daily life. You write emails, notes, and messages every day. Good writing helps you share your thoughts clearly with others. You do not need to be a great writer to start. Just write a few sentences about your day each evening. Over time, your writing will get much better and more natural.

Some people find it hard to make reading a habit. The key is to start small and enjoy the process. You can begin with just five minutes of reading each day. Pick a topic that interests you and makes you curious. Short stories or easy articles are good choices for beginners. Do not choose books that are too long or too difficult at first.

The same idea works for writing habits too. Set a simple goal and write a little bit every day. You can keep a small diary or journal on your desk. Write about what you did, what you saw, or how you felt. Do not worry about making mistakes in your writing. The goal is just to practice and build confidence over time.

Reading and writing are closely connected to each other. When you read more, you learn new words and sentence patterns. These words and patterns will appear in your own writing later. Many great writers say that reading is the first step to good writing. You cannot write well if you never read other people's work. Reading gives you examples and ideas to use in your own writing.

Technology has made reading and writing easier than before. You can use your phone or computer to read articles online. Many apps help you track your reading time and progress. You can also write on a laptop or tablet instead of paper. Some apps even check your grammar and spelling for you. These tools make learning more convenient and fun for everyone.

Building good habits takes time and patience. Start reading and writing today, even just a little. Do not expect to see results right away in the beginning. Keep going and trust the process of daily practice. After a few weeks, you will notice real improvements. Reading and writing will become a natural part of your daily routine.

三、语法简析

"one of the best ways" — 最高级前用 one of

"one of the + 最高级 + 名词复数" 表示"最……之一"，是英语中非常高频的表达。

原句： Reading is one of the best ways to learn new things every day.

结构： one of the + best(最高级) + ways(名词复数) + to do(不定式)

练习造句：

  • Traveling is one of the most exciting ways to learn about other cultures.（旅行是了解其他文化的最令人兴奋的方式之一。）
  • Watching movies is one of the easiest ways to practice listening.（看电影是练习听力最简单的方式之一。）

"find it hard to do" — it 作形式宾语

当宾语是不定式短语时，用 it 作形式宾语放在动词后面，真正的宾语（to do）放在后面。

原句： Some people find it hard to make reading a habit.

结构： find + it(形式宾语) + hard(宾语补足语) + to do(真正宾语)

练习造句：

  • I find it easy to learn English with movies.（我觉得用电影学英语很容易。）
  • She finds it difficult to wake up early in the morning.（她觉得早上早起很难。）

"instead of" — 表示"而不是"

instead of 是介词短语，后面接名词、代词或动名词（doing），表示"而不是、替代"。

原句： You can also write on a laptop or tablet instead of paper.

结构： 动词 + instead of + 名词/动名词

练习造句：

  • I walked to school instead of taking the bus.（我走路去学校，而不是坐公交车。）
  • She drinks tea instead of coffee in the morning.（她早上喝茶而不是喝咖啡。）
