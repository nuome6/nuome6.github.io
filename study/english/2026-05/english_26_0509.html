喝水的重要性

Nuouo2026/5/9大约 4 分钟英语学习每日英语基础英语

喝水的重要性

一、英文原文

Water is the most important drink in the world. Our body is about 60 percent water. Every cell in our body needs water to work properly.

When we don't drink enough water, our body becomes dehydrated. Dehydration can cause headaches, tiredness, and dry skin. It can also make it hard to think clearly.

Drinking water has many benefits. First, it helps our brain work better. Studies show that even a small amount of dehydration can affect our mood and memory. Second, water helps carry nutrients to different parts of our body. Our blood is more than 90 percent water, and blood brings oxygen and food to every cell.

Third, water helps keep our joints healthy. The cartilage in our joints contains about 80 percent water. When we are well-hydrated, our joints move more easily and feel less pain.

How much water should we drink? Experts say most people need about 8 glasses of water each day. But the amount depends on your body size, how active you are, and the weather. If you exercise or it is hot outside, you need more.

A simple tip: carry a water bottle with you. When you feel thirsty, drink water instead of soda or juice. Your body will thank you.

三、语法简析

最高级 the most + 形容词

Water is the most important drink in the world.

  • 最高级前要加 the
  • 用于三者或三者以上的比较
  • 常见搭配：the most + 多音节形容词

练习：

  • 这是最有趣的一本书 → This is the most interesting book.
  • 她是最聪明的学生 → She is the most intelligent student.

make it + 形容词 + to do

Dehydration can make it hard to think clearly.

  • make it hard/difficult/easy to do — 使做某事变得困难/容易
  • it 是形式宾语，真正的宾语是后面的不定式 to do

练习：

  • 这个问题让我很难回答 → This question makes it hard for me to answer.
  • 运动让身体更容易保持健康 → Exercise makes it easy to stay healthy.

depend on 取决于

The amount depends on your body size.

  • depend on = 取决于
  • 后面可以接名词、代词或从句

练习：

  • 价格取决于质量 → The price depends on the quality.
  • 结果取决于你多努力 → The result depends on how hard you work.

C. Headaches and tiredness
D. Stronger joints

A. 4 glasses
B. 6 glasses
C. 8 glasses
D. 10 glasses

4. What should you drink when you feel thirsty?

A. Soda
B. Juice
C. Milk
D. Water
