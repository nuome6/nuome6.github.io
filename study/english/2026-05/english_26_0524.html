安全与急救常识

安全与急救常识

一、英文原文

Safety and First Aid Basics

Safety is very important in our daily lives. We should always be careful at home and outside. Knowing a few safety rules can save lives. Here are some basic tips for staying safe.

Fire is one of the most dangerous things at home. If you see a fire, leave the building right away. Call one one nine and do not stop to take your things.

Sometimes we cut our fingers while cooking in the kitchen. First, wash the wound with clean water. Then, put a bandage on it gently to stop the bleeding.

If a person cannot breathe, they may be choking. Stand behind them and press on their stomach hard. This helps push the food out so they can breathe.

Crossing the road can be dangerous for children and adults. Always look both ways before you walk across the street. Follow the traffic lights and use the sidewalk for safety.

Every family should know important emergency phone numbers by heart. In China, one one nine is for fire and one two zero is for an ambulance. Stay calm and describe your location clearly when you call for help.

Learning first aid is not difficult, but it is very useful. It helps us protect ourselves and save others in danger. Share these safety tips with your family and friends today.

（共 211 词）

三、语法简析

if 条件句

"if + 一般现在时，主句用一般现在时/祈使句"表示真实条件。

原句： If you see a fire, leave the building right away.

结构： if（如果）+ you see a fire（条件从句）+ leave the building right away（祈使句主句）

练习造句：

  • If it rains, take an umbrella with you.（如果下雨，带把伞。）
  • If you feel sick, go to the doctor.（如果你感到不舒服，去看医生。）

must / should + 动词原形表示建议和义务

"should/must + 动词原形"用于表达建议、义务或必要性。

原句： We should always be careful at home and outside.

结构： should（应该）+ always be careful（动词短语）+ at home and outside（地点状语）

练习造句：

  • You should drink more water every day.（你应该每天多喝水。）
  • We must follow the traffic rules.（我们必须遵守交通规则。）

动名词作主语

动词-ing形式（如 knowing, crossing, learning）可以放在句首作主语。

原句： Knowing a few safety rules can save lives.

结构： Knowing a few safety rules（动名词短语作主语）+ can save（谓语）+ lives（宾语）

练习造句：

  • Learning a new language takes time.（学习一门新语言需要时间。）
  • Reading books helps us grow.（读书帮助我们成长。）

四、阅读理解

1. What should you do first if you see a fire at home?

A. Call your parents
B. Leave the building right away
C. Try to put out the fire
D. Open all the windows

2. How do you help a person who is choking?

A. Give them water to drink
B. Pat them on the back gently
C. Press on their stomach hard
D. Let them lie down and rest

3. What number should you call for an ambulance in China?

A. 110
B. 114
C. 119
D. 120

4. What is the main idea of the article?

A. Fire is the only danger at home
B. First aid is difficult to learn
C. Safety rules and first aid are important and useful
D. Only doctors can help injured people

答案：1.B 2.C 3.D 4.C
