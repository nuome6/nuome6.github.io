科技如何帮助我们的日常生活

Nuouo2026/5/11大约 6 分钟英语学习每日英语基础英语

科技如何帮助我们的日常生活

一、英文原文

How Technology Helps Us Every Day

Technology is all around us every day. It makes our daily life much easier. We use many devices without even thinking.

Smartphones are very powerful tools. We use them to call our friends and family. We also send messages and take photos. Many people use phones to pay for things in stores.

The internet helps us learn new things. We can search for information about any topic. Many students take online classes at home. It is like having a big library in our pocket.

Computers help us with work and study. We write emails and make reports on them. We can also draw and design on computers. Some people even make videos and music with computers.

Smart home devices save us a lot of time. We can turn on lights with our voice. Robot vacuums clean the floor by themselves. Smart thermostats keep our home at the right temperature.

GPS and map apps help us travel. We never get lost with our phones. We can find new restaurants and parks to explore. Map apps show us the fastest route to any place.

Technology keeps us connected to other people. We can see our family on video calls. We make new friends from different countries. Technology makes the world feel smaller and closer.

（约 220 词）

二、逐句精读

第一段

它让我们的日常生活变得更加轻松。

我们在不知不觉中使用许多设备。

智能手机是非常强大的工具。

互联网帮助我们学习新事物。

三、语法简析

help sb. do sth.（帮助某人做某事）

这是一个非常实用的句型，表示"帮助某人做某事"。

原句： The internet helps us learn new things.

互联网帮助我们学习新事物。

结构： help + 某人 + 动词原形 + 其他

练习造句：

  • My mom helps me do my homework.（我妈妈帮我做作业。）
  • Maps help us find new places.（地图帮助我们找到新地方。）

use sth. to do sth.（使用某物做某事）

这个句型表示用某个工具或物品来完成某个动作。

原句： We use them to call our friends and family.

我们用它们给朋友和家人打电话。

结构： use + 某物 + to + 动词原形

练习造句：

  • I use a pen to write my name.（我用笔写我的名字。）
  • She uses her phone to take pictures.（她用手机拍照。）

keep + 宾语 + 形容词（保持……状态）

这个句型表示让某人或某物保持在某种状态。

原句： Smart thermostats keep our home at the right temperature.

智能恒温器让我们的家保持适宜的温度。

结构： keep + 名词/代词 + 形容词/介词短语

练习造句：

  • Exercise keeps my body healthy.（运动让我的身体保持健康。）
  • Music keeps me happy when I study.（学习时音乐让我保持快乐。）

四、阅读理解

1. What does the article say about smartphones?

A. They are only for calling people
B. They are very powerful tools
C. They are too expensive to buy
D. They cannot take photos

2. How does the internet help students according to the article?

A. It helps them play games
B. It helps them watch movies
C. It helps them take online classes at home
D. It helps them buy things

3. What can robot vacuums do?

A. They can cook food
B. They can wash clothes
C. They can clean the floor by themselves
D. They can turn on lights

4. What is the main idea of this article?

A. Technology is too difficult to use
B. Smartphones are the only useful device
C. Technology makes our daily life easier in many ways
D. We should use less technology

答案：1.B 2.C 3.C 4.C
