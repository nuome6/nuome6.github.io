社区与志愿服务的意义

社区与志愿服务的意义

一、英文原文

The Meaning of Community and Volunteering

A community is a group of people living in the same area. Communities can be small villages or large cities. People in a community share common spaces like parks and schools. A strong community helps everyone feel safe and happy.

Volunteering means helping others without expecting money. Many people volunteer in their local communities every week. You can volunteer at a hospital, a school, or a food bank. Volunteering brings people together and builds trust.

When you volunteer, you help people who need support. Older people may need someone to talk to or help with shopping. Children may need tutors to help them with their homework. Your small act of kindness can change someone's whole day.

Volunteering also helps you learn new skills and make friends. You can improve your communication and teamwork abilities. Studies show that volunteers feel happier and less stressed. Helping others gives you a sense of purpose and pride.

There are many ways to volunteer in your community. You can clean up a park or plant trees along the street. You can visit hospitals to read books to patients. Some volunteers help at animal shelters or community gardens.

Starting to volunteer is easier than you think. You can ask at your school or look online for local groups. Even one hour a week can make a big difference. The important thing is to start with something you enjoy.

A great community needs people who care about each other. Volunteering is one of the best ways to show you care. When everyone helps, the whole community becomes stronger. So find a cause you love and start volunteering today.

二、逐句精读

第一段

A community is a group of people living in the same area.

社区是一群住在同一地区的人。

Communities can be small villages or large cities.

社区可以是小村庄，也可以是大城市。

People in a community share common spaces like parks and schools.

社区里的人共享公园和学校等公共空间。

A strong community helps everyone feel safe and happy.

一个强大的社区能让每个人都感到安全和快乐。

单词词性释义
communityn.社区
communitiesn.社区（复数）
commonadj.共同的，公共的

第二段

Volunteering means helping others without expecting money.

志愿服务意味着不求回报地帮助他人。

Many people volunteer in their local communities every week.

很多人每周都在自己所在的社区做志愿服务。

You can volunteer at a hospital, a school, or a food bank.

你可以在医院、学校或食物银行做志愿者。

Volunteering brings people together and builds trust.

志愿服务把人们聚在一起，建立起信任。

单词词性释义
volunteeringn.志愿服务
expectingv.期望，期待
localadj.当地的，本地的
trustn.信任

第三段

When you volunteer, you help people who need support.

当你做志愿者时，你在帮助需要支持的人。

Older people may need someone to talk to or help with shopping.

老年人可能需要有人聊天或帮忙购物。

Children may need tutors to help them with their homework.

孩子们可能需要家教来帮他们做作业。

Your small act of kindness can change someone's whole day.

你一个小小的善举就能改变某人的一整天。

单词词性释义
supportn.支持
olderadj.年长的
kindnessn.善良，善举

第四段

Volunteering also helps you learn new skills and make friends.

志愿服务还能帮你学习新技能和交朋友。

You can improve your communication and teamwork abilities.

你可以提高你的沟通和团队合作能力。

Studies show that volunteers feel happier and less stressed.

研究表明，志愿者感到更快乐、压力更小。

Helping others gives you a sense of purpose and pride.

帮助他人会给你一种目标感和自豪感。

单词词性释义
improvev.提高，改善
communicationn.沟通，交流
teamworkn.团队合作
stressedadj.有压力的
purposen.目的，目标
priden.自豪，骄傲

第五段

There are many ways to volunteer in your community.

在你的社区里有很多做志愿服务的方式。

You can clean up a park or plant trees along the street.

你可以清理公园或在街道旁种树。

You can visit hospitals to read books to patients.

你可以去医院给病人读书。

Some volunteers help at animal shelters or community gardens.

有些志愿者在动物收容所或社区花园帮忙。

单词词性释义
clean upphr.清理
patientsn.病人
sheltersn.收容所
gardensn.花园

第六段

Starting to volunteer is easier than you think.

开始做志愿者比你想象的要简单。

You can ask at your school or look online for local groups.

你可以在学校询问，或者在网上查找当地的团体。

Even one hour a week can make a big difference.

即使每周只花一个小时也能产生很大变化。

The important thing is to start with something you enjoy.

重要的是从你喜欢的事情开始。

单词词性释义
enjoyv.享受，喜欢

第七段

A great community needs people who care about each other.

一个伟大的社区需要互相关心的人。

Volunteering is one of the best ways to show you care.

志愿服务是表达关心的最好方式之一。

When everyone helps, the whole community becomes stronger.

当每个人都伸出援手，整个社区就会变得更强大。

So find a cause you love and start volunteering today.

所以，找到一个你热爱的事业，今天就开始做志愿者吧。

单词词性释义
strongeradj.更强大的
causen.事业，目标

三、语法简析

动名词作主语

动名词（动词 + ing）可以像名词一样放在句首当主语。

原句： Volunteering means helping others without expecting money.

结构： Volunteering（动名词作主语）+ means（谓语）+ helping others（动名词作宾语）。

练习造句：

  • Reading books is a great way to learn new things.（读书是学习新事物的好方法。）
  • Swimming helps you stay healthy and strong.（游泳帮助你保持健康和强壮。）

比较级 + than 表示"比……更"

用 "比较级 + than" 来比较两件事物。

原句： Starting to volunteer is easier than you think.

结构： Starting to volunteer（动名词短语作主语）+ is easier than（比较级）+ you think（比较对象）。

练习造句：

  • This book is more interesting than that one.（这本书比那本更有趣。）
  • Running is faster than walking.（跑步比走路更快。）

定语从句修饰人或物

用 who 修饰人，用 which/that 修饰事物。

原句： A great community needs people who care about each other.

结构： people（先行词）+ who care about each other（定语从句，修饰 people）。

练习造句：

  • She is a teacher who loves her students very much.（她是一位非常爱学生的老师。）
  • This is the book that changed my life.（这是改变了我人生的那本书。）

四、阅读理解

1. What does "volunteering" mean in the article?

A. Working for money in a community
B. Helping others without expecting money
C. Going to school every day
D. Playing sports with friends

2. How can volunteering help YOU?

A. It makes you richer
B. It teaches you new skills and makes you happier
C. It gives you free food
D. It helps you sleep better

3. What is one way to start volunteering?

A. Buy expensive equipment
B. Ask at your school or look online for local groups
C. Move to a new city
D. Stop going to class

4. According to the article, what makes a community stronger?

A. When everyone builds big houses
B. When people pay more money
C. When everyone helps each other
D. When there are more schools

答案：1.B 2.B 3.B 4.C
