保护我们的自然环境

Nuouo2026/5/14大约 7 分钟英语学习每日英语基础英语

保护我们的自然环境

一、英文原文

Our planet has many beautiful natural places. Forests, rivers, and mountains are all part of nature. These places give us clean air and fresh water. They also provide homes for animals and plants. Without nature, life on Earth cannot survive.

However, many natural places are in danger today. Factories produce dirty air and waste water. Cars and trucks release harmful pollution into the sky. Rivers and oceans become dirty from garbage. Trees are cut down every day. These actions hurt our environment badly.

One big problem is too much plastic waste. People use plastic bags, bottles, and boxes every day. Most of this plastic goes into the ocean. Marine animals sometimes eat the plastic by mistake. This can make them very sick or even kill them.

Another problem is losing our forests. Trees absorb carbon dioxide and produce oxygen. When we cut down trees, we get less clean air. Many species of animals also lose their homes when forests disappear. Some animals may even become extinct.

What can we do to help? First, we should reduce the waste we produce. We can use cloth bags instead of plastic ones. We should also drink from reusable water bottles. These small changes make a big difference.

Second, we should protect our wildlife. We can learn about local animals and plants. We should never buy products made from endangered species. Joining a nature group is also a good idea. Together, we can do more than alone.

The future of our planet depends on our actions today. Every person can help protect nature. By making simple choices every day, we can keep our world green and beautiful. Let us start now.

（共 208 词）

二、逐句精读

第一段

Our planet has many beautiful natural places.

我们的星球有许多美丽的大自然景观。

Forests, rivers, and mountains are all part of nature.

森林、河流和山脉都是大自然的一部分。

These places give us clean air and fresh water.

这些地方为我们提供洁净的空气和新鲜的水。

They also provide homes for animals and plants.

它们也为动物和植物提供栖息地。

Without nature, life on Earth cannot survive.

没有大自然，地球上的生命就无法生存。

单词词性释义
naturaladj.自然的，天然的
survivev.生存，存活

第二段

However, many natural places are in danger today.

然而，今天许多自然地方正面临危险。

Factories produce dirty air and waste water.

工厂排放脏空气和废水。

Cars and trucks release harmful pollution into the sky.

汽车和卡车将有害污染排放到空中。

Rivers and oceans become dirty from garbage.

河流和海洋因垃圾而变脏。

Trees are cut down every day.

树木每天都在被砍伐。

These actions hurt our environment badly.

这些行为严重伤害了我们的环境。

单词词性释义
dangern.危险
pollutionn.污染
garbagen.垃圾
hurtv.伤害，损害

第三段

One big problem is too much plastic waste.

一个大问题是太多的塑料垃圾。

People use plastic bags, bottles, and boxes every day.

人们每天都使用塑料袋、塑料瓶和塑料盒。

Most of this plastic goes into the ocean.

这些塑料大部分流入了海洋。

Marine animals sometimes eat the plastic by mistake.

海洋动物有时会误食塑料。

This can make them very sick or even kill them.

这会让它们生重病，甚至死亡。

单词词性释义
plasticn./adj.塑料（的）
marineadj.海洋的

第四段

Another problem is losing our forests.

另一个问题是失去我们的森林。

Trees absorb carbon dioxide and produce oxygen.

树木吸收二氧化碳并产生氧气。

When we cut down trees, we get less clean air.

当我们砍倒树木时，我们得到的洁净空气就少了。

Many species of animals also lose their homes when forests disappear.

当森林消失时，许多动物物种也失去了家园。

Some animals may even become extinct.

有些动物甚至可能灭绝。

单词词性释义
forestn.森林
absorbv.吸收
speciesn.物种
extinctadj.灭绝的

第五段

What can we do to help?

我们能做些什么来帮忙呢？

First, we should reduce the waste we produce.

首先，我们应该减少产生的垃圾。

We can use cloth bags instead of plastic ones.

我们可以使用布袋来代替塑料袋。

We should also drink from reusable water bottles.

我们也应该使用可重复使用的水瓶喝水。

These small changes make a big difference.

这些小小的改变会带来很大的不同。

单词词性释义
reducev.减少
reusableadj.可重复使用的

第六段

Second, we should protect our wildlife.

其次，我们应该保护我们的野生动物。

We can learn about local animals and plants.

我们可以了解当地的动物和植物。

We should never buy products made from endangered species.

我们绝不应该购买来自濒危物种的产品。

Joining a nature group is also a good idea.

加入一个自然环保组织也是个好主意。

Together, we can do more than alone.

团结起来，我们能做的事比独自一人多得多。

单词词性释义
wildlifen.野生动物
endangeredadj.濒危的

第七段

The future of our planet depends on our actions today.

我们星球的未来取决于我们今天的行动。

Every person can help protect nature.

每个人都可以帮助保护大自然。

By making simple choices every day, we can keep our world green and beautiful.

通过每天做出简单的选择，我们可以让我们的世界保持绿色和美丽。

Let us start now.

让我们现在就开始吧。

单词词性释义
dependv.取决于，依赖
simpleadj.简单的

三、语法简析

被动语态

被动语态用于强调动作的承受者，而不是执行者。常见结构：主语 + be + 过去分词。

原句： Trees are cut down every day.

结构： Trees（主语）+ are cut down（被动语态）+ every day（时间状语）

练习造句：

  • The homework is done by students every evening.（作业每天晚上由学生完成。）
  • The cake was eaten by the children.（蛋糕被孩子们吃掉了。）

instead of 的用法

instead of 表示"代替、而不是"，后面接名词、代词或动名词。

原句： We can use cloth bags instead of plastic ones.

结构： 主语 + 动词 + A + instead of + B

练习造句：

  • She reads books instead of watching TV.（她读书而不是看电视。）
  • We can walk instead of driving.（我们可以步行而不是开车。）

by + 动名词 表示方式

by + 动名词可以表示"通过做某事"来实现某个目的。

原句： By making simple choices every day, we can keep our world green and beautiful.

结构： By + 动名词短语, 主语 + 动词

练习造句：

  • By practicing every day, you can improve your English.（通过每天练习，你可以提高英语。）
  • By eating vegetables, we stay healthy.（通过吃蔬菜，我们保持健康。）

四、阅读理解

1. What do forests, rivers, and mountains give us?

A. Money and food
B. Clean air and fresh water
C. Technology and machines
D. Clothes and houses

2. Why is plastic waste a problem for marine animals?

A. They like the taste of plastic
B. They sometimes eat plastic by mistake
C. Plastic makes the water warm
D. Plastic blocks the sunlight

3. What happens when forests disappear?

A. The weather gets hotter everywhere
B. Animals lose their homes and some become extinct
C. More factories are built
D. People become healthier

4. What is one thing we can do to help?

A. Use more plastic bags
B. Cut down more trees
C. Buy products made from endangered species
D. Use cloth bags instead of plastic ones

答案：1.B 2.B 3.B 4.D
