睡眠的重要性

Nuouo2026/5/12大约 6 分钟英语学习每日英语基础英语

睡眠的重要性

一、英文原文

The Importance of Sleep

Sleep is very important for our body. Our body needs rest every night. When we sleep, our body gets stronger. Good sleep helps us think clearly. A tired mind makes many mistakes.

Children need about ten hours of sleep. Adults need seven to eight hours. Too little sleep makes us feel bad. We may feel angry or sad easily. Our body cannot work well without rest.

Our body repairs itself during sleep. Our brain saves new information. Dreams help us process our feelings. Sleep keeps us healthy and strong.

Some people have trouble falling asleep. They lie in bed and cannot rest. Phone screens can make sleep harder. The bright light stops our brain from resting. We should put phones away before bed.

Go to bed at the same time each night. Keep your room cool and dark. A warm bath can help you relax. Reading a book is a good habit.

Sleep helps students learn new things. Our brain practices what we studied. Good sleep improves our memory. Students who sleep well get better grades.

Make sleep part of your daily routine. Your body and mind will thank you. Sleep is a simple gift to yourself. Start tonight by going to bed early.

（共 210 词）

三、语法简析

过去分词作定语

当过去分词放在名词前面修饰名词时，表示被动或已完成的状态。例如 "tired mind" 中，"tired" 是过去分词作定语，意思是"疲惫的（被累的）大脑"。

原句： A tired mind makes many mistakes.

结构： 过去分词（tired）+ 名词（mind）= 形容词用法

练习造句：

  • I need a broken clock repaired.（我需要修理一个坏掉的钟。）
  • The excited children ran to the park.（兴奋的孩子们跑向了公园。）

"too...to" 结构的简化表达

英语中用 "too little" 表示"太少"，"too much" 表示"太多"。"Too little sleep makes us feel bad" 用了 "too + 形容词" 表示过度的程度。

原句： Too little sleep makes us feel bad.

结构： too + 形容词（little）+ 名词（sleep）+ 谓语

练习造句：

  • Too much sugar is not good for you.（太多糖对你不好。）
  • Too many people make the room noisy.（太多人让房间很吵。）

现在分词短语作后置定语

"falling asleep" 是现在分词短语，放在名词后面修饰它，表示动作正在进行或发生。"Trouble falling asleep" 意思是"入睡的困难"。

原句： Some people have trouble falling asleep.

结构： 名词（trouble）+ 现在分词短语（falling asleep）

练习造句：

  • I have difficulty waking up early.（我很难早起。）
  • She enjoys reading books before bed.（她喜欢睡前读书。）

四、阅读理解

1. How many hours of sleep do children need?

A. Five to six hours
B. Seven to eight hours
C. About ten hours
D. Twelve hours

2. What can make sleep harder?

A. Reading a book
B. Taking a warm bath
C. Phone screens
D. Going to bed early

3. What does our brain do during sleep?

A. It forgets everything
B. It saves new information
C. It stops working
D. It watches TV

4. Why is good sleep important for students?

A. It helps them play games
B. It makes them taller
C. It improves their memory and grades
D. It gives them more free time

答案：1.C 2.C 3.B 4.C
