动物与生态

一、英文原文

The Secret Life of Bees

Bees are very small insects, but they do a very big job. Every day, bees fly from flower to flower. They collect nectar from the flowers. This nectar becomes honey in the hive. Bees also carry pollen on their legs. This pollen helps new plants grow.

A single bee cannot live alone. It needs a colony of other bees. A colony can have thousands of bees. The queen bee is the leader. She lays all the eggs. Worker bees bring food and protect the queen. Without the queen, the colony will die.

Flowers need bees to reproduce. When bees visit flowers, pollen moves from one flower to another. This is called pollination. About seventy percent of our food comes from plants that need bees. Apples, strawberries, and almonds all need bees.

Sadly, bee populations are falling fast. Pesticides in farms are very harmful to bees. Some chemicals kill bees directly. Others make bees too weak to fly. When bees die, our food supply is at risk.

Climate change is also a big problem for bees. Flowers bloom at the wrong time because the weather is strange. Bees may wake up before flowers are ready. Or flowers may bloom before bees come out. This mismatch is dangerous for both.

Many people are trying to help bees now. You can plant flowers in your garden. You can avoid using chemicals on your lawn. You can even build a small bee hotel. These small actions help bees survive and keep our world beautiful.

Protecting bees is protecting ourselves. Bees and humans need each other. When we take care of nature, nature takes care of us. We must act now before it is too late.

（共 218 词）

二、逐句精读

第一段

Bees are very small insects, but they do a very big job.

蜜蜂是非常小的昆虫，但它们做着非常重要的工作。

Every day, bees fly from flower to flower.

每天，蜜蜂在花丛中飞来飞去。

They collect nectar from the flowers.

它们从花朵中采集花蜜。

This nectar becomes honey in the hive.

这些花蜜在蜂巢里变成蜂蜜。

Bees also carry pollen on their legs.

蜜蜂的腿上还带着花粉。

This pollen helps new plants grow.

这些花粉帮助新的植物生长。

单词词性释义
insectn.昆虫
nectarn.花蜜
hiven.蜂巢
pollenn.花粉

第二段

A single bee cannot live alone.

一只蜜蜂不能独自生存。

It needs a colony of other bees.

它需要一个由其他蜜蜂组成的群体。

A colony can have thousands of bees.

一个蜂群可以有成千上万只蜜蜂。

The queen bee is the leader.

蜂后是领导者。

She lays all the eggs.

她产下所有的卵。

Worker bees bring food and protect the queen.

工蜂负责带来食物并保护蜂后。

Without the queen, the colony will die.

没有蜂后，蜂群就会死亡。

单词词性释义
colonyn.群体，蜂群
queenn.蜂后，女王
workern.工蜂，工人

第三段

Flowers need bees to reproduce.

花朵需要蜜蜂来繁殖。

When bees visit flowers, pollen moves from one flower to another.

当蜜蜂拜访花朵时，花粉从一朵花传到另一朵花。

This is called pollination.

这叫做授粉。

About seventy percent of our food comes from plants that need bees.

我们大约百分之七十的食物来自需要蜜蜂的植物。

Apples, strawberries, and almonds all need bees.

苹果、草莓和杏仁都需要蜜蜂。

单词词性释义
reproducev.繁殖
pollinationn.授粉
almondn.杏仁

第四段

Sadly, bee populations are falling fast.

遗憾的是，蜜蜂数量正在急剧下降。

Pesticides in farms are very harmful to bees.

农场里的杀虫剂对蜜蜂非常有害。

Some chemicals kill bees directly.

一些化学物质直接杀死蜜蜂。

Others make bees too weak to fly.

另一些让蜜蜂虚弱到无法飞行。

When bees die, our food supply is at risk.

当蜜蜂死亡时，我们的食物供应就面临危险。

单词词性释义
populationn.数量，种群
pesticiden.杀虫剂
chemicaln.化学物质

第五段

Climate change is also a big problem for bees.

气候变化也是蜜蜂面临的一个大问题。

Flowers bloom at the wrong time because the weather is strange.

因为天气反常，花朵在错误的时间开放。

Bees may wake up before flowers are ready.

蜜蜂可能在花朵准备好之前就醒了。

Or flowers may bloom before bees come out.

或者花朵可能在蜜蜂出来之前就开了。

This mismatch is dangerous for both.

这种不匹配对两者都很危险。

单词词性释义
bloomv.开花
mismatchn.不匹配，错位

第六段

Many people are trying to help bees now.

现在很多人正在努力帮助蜜蜂。

You can plant flowers in your garden.

你可以在花园里种花。

You can avoid using chemicals on your lawn.

你可以避免在草坪上使用化学药剂。

You can even build a small bee hotel.

你甚至可以建一个小型蜜蜂旅馆。

These small actions help bees survive and keep our world beautiful.

这些小行动帮助蜜蜂生存，也让我们的世界保持美丽。

单词词性释义
lawnn.草坪
survivev.生存
beautifuladj.美丽的

第七段

Protecting bees is protecting ourselves.

保护蜜蜂就是保护我们自己。

Bees and humans need each other.

蜜蜂和人类互相需要。

When we take care of nature, nature takes care of us.

当我们照顾大自然时，大自然也会照顾我们。

We must act now before it is too late.

我们必须现在就行动，趁还来得及。

单词词性释义
protectv.保护
actv.行动

三、语法简析

现在进行时

表示正在进行的动作或目前阶段的状态。结构：主语 + am/is/are + 动词-ing。

原句： Bee populations are falling fast.

结构： 主语(bee populations) + are + falling(现在分词) + fast

练习造句：

  • The children are playing in the park.（孩子们正在公园里玩耍。）
  • She is reading a book right now.（她现在正在读一本书。）

too...to... 结构

表示"太……以至于不能……"。too 后接形容词或副词，to 后接动词原形。

原句： Others make bees too weak to fly.

结构： make + 宾语(bees) + too + 形容词(weak) + to + 动词原形(fly)

练习造句：

  • The box is too heavy to carry.（这个箱子太重了，搬不动。）
  • He is too young to drive.（他太小了，不能开车。）

主语从句

用动名词短语或 that 从句作主语。动名词短语作主语时，谓语动词用单数。

原句： Protecting bees is protecting ourselves.

结构： Protecting bees(动名词短语作主语) + is(单数谓语) + protecting ourselves

练习造句：

  • Learning English is not easy.（学英语并不容易。）
  • Reading before bed helps you sleep.（睡前阅读有助于睡眠。）

四、阅读理解

1. What do bees collect from flowers?

A. Water
B. Nectar
C. Seeds
D. Leaves

2. What is the role of the queen bee?

A. She makes honey
B. She flies to collect pollen
C. She lays all the eggs
D. She protects the hive from rain

3. What is one reason bee populations are falling?

A. Too many flowers
B. Pesticides in farms
C. Too much honey
D. Bees are too big

4. What can people do to help bees?

A. Plant flowers and avoid chemicals
B. Build tall buildings
C. Use more pesticides
D. Cut down trees

答案：1.B 2.C 3.B 4.A
