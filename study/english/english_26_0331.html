海洋物种：在被发现之前就已消亡

来源： ScienceDaily, Ocean species are disappearing before scientists can even find them | 2026年3月26日
原文链接： https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/03/260326075603.htm
题材： 生态与环境 · 适合考研英语阅读

一、英文原文

Species around the world are vanishing at an accelerating pace, driven by climate change, habitat destruction, and the spread of invasive species. Nowhere is this crisis more acute than in the oceans, where marine organisms are disappearing before scientists can even catalogue them. An international team of researchers from the University of Göttingen, the Leibniz Institute for Biodiversity Change Analysis, and the Senckenberg Society for Nature Research is racing against time to address this gap. They are building EuroWorm, a massive open-access genomic database designed to document European marine annelids — segmented sea worms — and make the data freely available to scientists worldwide. "By comparing data on European species, we hope to accelerate the discovery of new species and biodiversity research worldwide — and thus counteract the 'silent extinction' of marine species," explains project leader Dr. Jenna Moore. The loss of marine biodiversity is not merely an ecological concern; it weakens the ocean ecosystem's ability to withstand disturbances, adapt to climate change, and fulfill its role as a global climate regulator. As ocean ecosystems become increasingly depleted, they grow ever more vulnerable to the very changes that are destroying them — a vicious cycle that scientists are determined to break.

三、全文翻译

全球范围内的物种正在以加速的节奏消亡，其驱动因素包括气候变化、栖息地破坏以及入侵物种的扩散。在海洋中，这场危机尤为严峻——海洋生物甚至在科学家能够对其编目之前就已消失。来自哥廷根大学、莱布尼茨生物多样性变化分析研究所和森肯伯格自然研究学会的一支国际研究团队正在争分夺秒地弥补这一空白。他们正在构建 EuroWorm——一个大型开放获取基因组数据库，旨在记录欧洲海洋环节动物（分节的海虫）的数据，并免费向全球科学家开放。项目负责人 Jenna Moore 博士解释道："通过比较欧洲物种的数据，我们希望能加速新物种的发现和全球生物多样性研究，从而遏制海洋物种的'无声灭绝'。"海洋生物多样性的丧失不仅仅是一个生态问题——它削弱了海洋生态系统抵御干扰、适应气候变化以及发挥全球气候调节器作用的能力。随着海洋生态系统日益枯竭，它们对正在摧毁它们的那些变化变得更加脆弱——这是一个科学家们决心打破的恶性循环。

五、语法简析

句型一：Nowhere is ... more ... than ...（倒装句 + 比较级）

Nowhere is this crisis more acute than in the oceans, where marine organisms are disappearing before scientists can even catalogue them.

结构分析：

  • 否定副词 Nowhere 置于句首，主谓部分倒装（is this crisis 而非 this crisis is）
  • "否定词 + 比较级" 等于最高级含义："Nowhere is more acute than" = "在海洋中最为严峻"
  • where 引导非限制性定语从句，修饰 the oceans

考研重点： 否定词位于句首的倒装结构是考研英语常见考点。常见否定词有：never, seldom, rarely, hardly, scarcely, nowhere, little, not only 等。

句型二：not merely ... ; it ...（分号连接的并列句 + 排比结构）

The loss of marine biodiversity is not merely an ecological concern**;** it weakens the ocean ecosystem's ability to withstand disturbances, adapt to climate change, and fulfill its role as a global climate regulator.

结构分析：

  • 分号连接两个独立分句，形成递进关系（"不仅仅是A；还B"）
  • "not merely" 表示"不仅仅"，后半句用分号引出更重要的信息
  • to withstand..., adapt..., and fulfill... 是三个并列的不定式短语，共同修饰 ability

考研重点： "not merely/only" 常与 "but also" 搭配，但此处使用分号分隔，使语气更强烈。排比结构（三个不定式并列）增强表达力度，是写作中值得模仿的句式。
