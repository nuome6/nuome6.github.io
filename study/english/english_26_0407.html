早餐为什么重要

日期： 2026年4月7日
题材： 健康与生活习惯 · 适合基础英语阅读

一、英文原文

"Have breakfast like a king, lunch like a prince, and dinner like a poor man." You may have heard this old saying. It means breakfast is the most important meal of the day. But why?

When you wake up in the morning, your body has not had any food for about eight to ten hours. Your energy is low, and your brain needs fuel to start working. Breakfast gives you that energy.

Studies show that people who eat breakfast do better in school and at work. They can think more clearly and remember things more easily. For students, this is especially important before a difficult class or an exam.

But many young people still skip breakfast. Some say they have no time in the morning. Others say they are not hungry. Some just want to sleep a few more minutes.

So what makes a good breakfast? You don't need something expensive or complicated. A simple bowl of rice porridge and an egg is already a good start. If you have more time, you can add some fruit or a glass of milk. The key is to eat something — even a small piece of bread is better than nothing.

Doctors also suggest that breakfast should include some protein, like eggs or fish. Protein helps you feel full for longer. That means you won't feel hungry again before lunch.

Next time your alarm rings, try getting up five minutes earlier. Make yourself a simple breakfast. Your body and your brain will thank you.

三、全文翻译

"早餐要吃得像国王，午餐吃得像王子，晚餐吃得像穷人。"你可能听过这句老话。它的意思是早餐是一天中最重要的一餐。但为什么呢？

当你早上醒来时，你的身体已经有大约八到十个小时没有进食了。你的能量很低，大脑需要燃料才能开始工作。早餐就给你提供这种能量。

研究表明，吃早餐的人在工作和学习中表现更好。他们思考更清晰，记忆更容易。对学生来说，在一节很难的课或考试前吃早餐尤其重要。

但很多年轻人仍然不吃早餐。有人说早上没时间，有人说自己不饿，还有些人只想多睡几分钟。

那么什么才是一顿好的早餐呢？你不需要很贵或很复杂的东西。一碗白粥加一个鸡蛋就已经是一个好的开始了。如果你有更多时间，可以再加一点水果或一杯牛奶。关键是吃点什么——哪怕只是一小片面包也比什么都不吃强。

医生还建议早餐应该包含一些蛋白质，比如鸡蛋或鱼。蛋白质能让你更长时间不觉得饿。也就是说午饭前你不会感到饿。

下次闹钟响的时候，试着早起五分钟。给自己做一顿简单的早餐。你的身体和大脑都会感谢你的。

五、语法简析

1. 比较级与最高级

  • 最高级： the most important meal
  • 比较级： better, more clearly, more easily
  • 多音节词用 more + 原级，如 more important

2. Some... Others... 结构

Some say they have no time. Others say they are not hungry.

描述不同原因时的经典句型。

3. 现在完成时

Your body has not had any food for about eight to ten hours.

has/have + 过去分词 + for/since，表从过去持续到现在。
