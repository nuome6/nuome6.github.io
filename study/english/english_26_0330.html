🤖 《AI 时代的工作场所：人性化危机？》 — 2026-03-30

来源： Resume Now,《AI and Workplace Humanity Report》| 2026年3月11日
原文链接： https://www.resume-now.com/job-resources/careers/ai-workplace-humanity
题材： 科技与就业 · 适合考研英语阅读

一、英文原文

A new national survey of more than 1,000 employed U.S. adults suggests that the biggest impact of AI may not be about productivity gains, but about how the workplace feels. Resume Now's AI and Workplace Humanity Report finds that nearly two-thirds (63%) of workers expect AI to make the workplace feel less human this year, and 42% cite dehumanization of work as one of the biggest workforce issues linked to AI.

"When most workers say AI will make the workplace feel less human, it signals anxiety about critical thinking, surveillance, and connection," the report states. Workers primarily identify over-reliance on AI as the leading workforce problem, reflecting fears that increased automation could weaken critical thinking and other essential human skills. Workers rank job loss as the main AI risk this year, followed by worries about data misuse, privacy, and declining creativity and critical thinking — qualities that shape the human side of work.

Meanwhile, a separate global survey of more than 16,400 office workers across 16 countries by Gensler found that 30% of employees now qualify as AI Power Users, employees who regularly use AI tools in both their professional and personal lives. Compared to late adopters, AI power users spend less time working alone (37% of their workweek versus 42%) and more time learning (12% versus 8%) and socializing (11% versus 9%).

三、全文翻译

一项针对美国1000多名就业成年人的全国性调查显示，AI的最大影响可能不在于提升生产力，而在于改变工作场所的感受。Resume Now发布的《AI与职场人性化报告》发现，近三分之二（63%）的劳动者预计AI会在今年让工作场所"更缺乏人性化"，42%的人将"工作非人化"列为与AI相关的最大职场问题之一。

报告指出："当大多数劳动者说AI会让工作场所变得不那么人性化时，这反映出他们对批判性思维、监控和人际联结的焦虑。"劳动者将"过度依赖AI"视为首要职场问题，这一现象反映出他们的担忧：自动化程度的提高可能会削弱批判性思维及其他重要的人类技能。劳动者将失业列为今年AI带来的主要风险，其次是数据滥用、隐私泄露，以及批判性思维和创造力——这些塑造了工作中"人性化"一面的素质——的退化。

与此同时，Gensler对来自16个国家的超过16400名办公室员工进行的另一项全球调查显示，30%的员工现已属于"AI重度用户"，即在职业和私人生活中定期使用AI工具的人群。与AI的"后来者"相比，重度用户独自工作的时间更少（占工作周的37%，后者为42%），而用于学习（12%对8%）和社交（11%对9%）的时间则更多。

五、语法简析

句型一：suggest that...（宾语从句）

A new national survey suggests that the biggest impact of AI may not be about productivity gains, but about how the workplace feels.

结构分析：

  • 主句：survey suggests
  • that 引导宾语从句，作 suggests 的宾语
  • 从句中使用 "not ... but ..." 结构，意为"不是……而是……"

翻译要点： suggest 在此不是"建议"，而是"表明、暗示"，后接 that 宾语从句时，从句使用陈述语序。

句型二：find that...（宾语从句 + 分词结构）

Resume Now's Report finds that nearly two-thirds (63%) of workers expect AI to make the workplace feel less human this year, and 42% cite dehumanization of work as one of the biggest workforce issues linked to AI.

结构分析：

  • find 后接两个并列的 that 宾语从句
  • 第二个从句中：cite A as B（把A列为B），linked to AI 为过去分词短语作定语修饰 issues

翻译要点： "cite A as B" 意思是"把A称为B/列为B"，linked to AI 相当于 which is linked to AI，属于后置定语。
